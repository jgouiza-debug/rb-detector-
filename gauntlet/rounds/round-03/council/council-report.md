# Council report — round 03

## The state of the UI

Pip is now a real, coherent product with one loud defect. Every machine gate passes, no hard cap is triggered, and the systems underneath are genuinely engineered rather than lucky: six type sizes enforced by wiping Tailwind's `--text-*` namespace, seven spacing steps with zero 12px or 20px anywhere in `app/` or `components/`, a token file that lists forbidden foreground/background pairs and unit-tests that they fail, a reduced-motion path handled three separate ways, and copy that sounds like a specific companion ("noted quietly · no reply", "i'm a small app, not a person who can keep you safe right now"). The crisis flow is the best-built screen in the product. And then the app hands you the thing it is asking $4.99 for — the keepsake — and the keepsake is generated by `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts`, which titles your Monday "Hard, honestly, everything", glues a connective onto a clause that already starts with a conjunction ("Later on, but i made it through"), and closes every Heavy day with the same borrowed sentence. Four of the five advisors revised their scores downward in peer review after opening that one file, including both advisors who had filed zero red flags. Around it sit three smaller craft problems: onboarding and settings-subscription leave 200–270px voids in screens that are otherwise on-grid, the thread's `backdrop-blur-md` top bar lets a day divider ghost through under the wordmark while the sticky composer clips the newest bubble, and a declared radius ladder is bypassed 26 times by untouched Tailwind defaults. None of it is broken. All of it is between this build and a product a tired person would pay for at 1am.

## Rulings on the four claims put to the chair

1. **`scripted.ts` — fixed `closing[mood]` map and unstripped `BRIDGES` connective. UPHELD, both halves, in source and in `timeline-light-full.jpg`.** `closing` is declared `Record<MoodTag, string>` with exactly six entries; nothing varies it, so any two days sharing a mood render an identical closing sentence, and the screenshot puts two Heavy days (Sep 7 and Sep 1) on screen together. `bridge` is selected by `seed % BRIDGES.length` and concatenated straight onto `e2.text.trim()` with no test for a leading conjunction; the render shows "Later on, **but** i made it through, and i'm home now." and, five cards down, "After that, **but** i got through it". Two independent instances on a single capture. The builder should treat this as confirmed, not alleged. (Two further defects in the same function are worth fixing in the same pass: the noun-bag title, and `sentence()` capitalising only `charAt(0)`.)
2. **`StepShell.tsx` — `aria-valuenow` / rendered-width mismatch. UPHELD.** `aria-valuenow={step}` and `aria-valuemax={total}` announce `step/total` (25% on the name step) while `pct = Math.round(((step + 1) / (total + 1)) * 100)` drives the fill (40%). `aria-label={`step ${step} of ${total}`}` compounds it. Confirmed visually in `onboarding-name-light.jpg`, where the fill ends at ≈41.5% of the bar width. The endowed-progress design is deliberate and good — the ARIA simply was not updated to match it. Affects 3 of the 4 step screens.
3. **`SystemMoment.tsx` — missing `tap` class. UPHELD.** The `day_ready` Link is `className="mx-auto my-2 flex items-center gap-2 rounded-pill bg-surface-2 px-4 py-2 text-sm font-semibold text-fg animate-fade-up"` — `py-2` (8+8) plus a 20px `text-sm` line-box computes to ~36px. It is a one-off, not a policy: the "get help now" Link fifteen lines below it in the same file carries `tap`, and so does `PauseChip.tsx`. It also has no `active:` press state, unlike every other tappable surface. It does **not** overturn A2's PASS, because the fixture set never renders `day_ready`.
4. **Does the memory-card keepsake carry photos in any capture? No — it does not, in any of the 26.** The capability is fully built: `MemoryCard.tsx:36-38` renders `day.thumbs.slice(0, 2)`, `MemoryDetail.tsx:102-108` renders a two-column `data.photos` grid with real alt text, `lib/timeline/query.ts:48` populates `thumbs`, `app/api/memory/[date]/route.ts:40` filters sensitive media out of `photos`, and `Composer.tsx` supports upload. But `memory-card-light-full.jpg` and `memory-card-dark-full.jpg` are text-only, both timeline `-full` captures show "2 moments kept" with no thumbnails beside it, and no thread capture contains a `PhotoBubble`. **Ruling: this is a fixture/seed gap, not a code defect — so it is not a red flag — but it is scoring-relevant and it caps what C3 can be credited for.** The design spec asks the keepsake to carry photos; the council has been asked to judge the product's most emotional artifact with its most emotional element switched off, and three advisors said so independently. Seed at least one day with two photos before round 4, or the keepsake will keep being scored as a wall of generated text.
