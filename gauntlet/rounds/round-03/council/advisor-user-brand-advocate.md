## Red flags

- **timeline-light-full / timeline-dark-full: the five `CompactCard` titles** — generic/machine copy. "Walk, under, amber", "Quiet, evening, listened", "Missed, friends, texted", "Tried, recipe, worked", "Hard, honestly, everything". Five identical `Word, word, word` shapes stacked down the page, produced by `scripted.ts` `nouns.slice(0,3).join(", ")` whose STOP list misses "under", "honestly", "everything", "listened".
- **settings-subscription-light: secondary button "just paid? tap here"**, and **meditation-pause: the toggle "4-4-4-4 Box · switch rhythm"** — copy that belongs to any app / to a clinical one, not to Pip. The first is billing plumbing shown to a user who has never paid; the second is `BOX.label` from `lib/breath/patterns.ts` leaking a protocol name onto the calm screen.
- **thread-light / thread-dark: the top bar** — blur used because it's possible. `TopBar` is `bg-bg/90 backdrop-blur-md`, so a yellow user bubble ghosts through behind the "pip" wordmark at y≈40, and the same treatment on `Composer` (`bg-bg/95`) clips the last bubble at y≈775. (The blur on memory-locked-paywall is *not* flagged — there it is the lock itself and it earns its place.)
- **onboarding-welcome-light/dark and onboarding-notify-light: vertical balance** — floaty. 198px of empty ground below the last control on welcome with the CTA sitting at y≈566 of 844; ~260px between the last body line and the CTA on notify. Content is centred in the top three-quarters and the bottom quarter is nothing.

Checked and **not** found: unmotivated gradients (the only gradients are Pip's `sunlight→honey` radial body/glow in `PipMascot.tsx` defs, brand-permitted, plus a transparent→bg scrim on the locked card); untouched framework spacing (95% 8pt, off-grid values are 4px only, radii are a deliberate 0.875/1.125/1.25rem set); too many colours or fonts (3 families, 3 weights, 6 sizes, 21/21 palette screens); card-grid filler (the CompactCard run is the product's own list of days, not decoration); over-merchandised mascot (Pip appears on the thread avatar, onboarding, the WeekFlow line, the card signature and the paywall — restrained, per brand §3).

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Rewrite the memory, not the memory card.** In `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts::synthesizeDay`: drop `BRIDGES[]` entirely (a random connective glued onto a raw second entry produces "Later on, but i made it through"), sentence-case the whole reflection rather than only `charAt(0)`, and either kill the six-string `closing` map or make it one line drawn from the day's own words — two Heavy days one week apart currently end with the identical sentence. Replace the `nouns.slice(0,3).join(", ")` title with the day's first clause, trimmed ("a walk under the amber leaves"), or with no title at all — a bare serif date is better than "Walk, under, amber". This single file is what makes the $4.99 feel unearned.
2. **Give Pip its warmth back in dark mode, and move the timeline into the thumb zone.** `--night-bubble-pip: #33302a` reads grey next to `#FFCF4D` at 25.2% coverage; lift it toward a genuinely warm raised tone (roughly `#3A332A`+ with a hint of the honey hue) so the companion's voice is visibly warm, and add the `sunlight` glow behind the thread avatar so Pip glows on near-black instead of receding. Then take the timeline out of the top-right `BookHeart` and give it the two-item bottom nav `03-design-spec.md §2` specified — the payoff should not be the least reachable target on the phone.
3. **Make the pause screen one decision and the memory a real reveal.** On meditation-pause, demote "4-4-4-4 Box · switch rhythm" and "pause" behind a single quiet control (or drop the pattern picker to a Pip+ setting), leave "i feel ready" as the only pill, and swap its honey fill for the soft variant so Pip and the ring hold the focal point. Separately, make `keepsake-in` actually distinct from `fade-up` — right now it is 10px vs 6px over 300ms vs 240ms — and let the "your day is ready to look back on" moment arrive with a beat of its own; and fix the two chrome bleeds by padding the message list by the composer height and dropping `backdrop-blur-md` on the top bar in favour of a solid `bg-bg` with a hairline.
