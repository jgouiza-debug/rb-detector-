# Taste Critic — round 03

Could this only be Pip? Mostly yes — and that is what makes the tells sting. The chrome, the mascot, the buttons and the human-written copy have a voice ("keep it all · $4.99/mo", "not right now", "a whole week, kept.", "i'm a small app, not a person who can keep you safe right now"). Then the product hands you its most precious artifact, the keepsake, and the keepsake is where the machine shows through.

## Red flags
- timeline-light-full / timeline-dark-full: "earlier" card titles "Walk, under, amber", "Quiet, evening, listened", "Missed, friends, texted", "Tried, recipe, worked", "Hard, honestly, everything", and today's card seam "Later on, but i made it through" — generic/machine copy with no point of view, in the keepsake of all places.
- settings-subscription-light: five check-circle rows ("every day you've ever written, kept" …) — the pricing-page feature-list shape; grey checks for things the free user doesn't have is a growth-team pattern in Pip's clothing.
- thread-light / thread-dark: TopBar `bg-bg/90 backdrop-blur-md` lets the "Sat, Sep 5" day divider ghost through beneath the "pip" wordmark — blur that reads as a smudge, not a help.
- onboarding-welcome light/dark: CTA at y≈566 with ~200px of empty cream below while every sibling step anchors at the bottom; memory-card light/dark: 32px date widow "6, 2026" — wrong balance / floaty.
- None found for: default gradients (checked PipMascot radial = the brand's permitted sunlight→honey glow; locked-memory `from-transparent to-bg` is a functional fade; no page-level gradients in globals.css), default framework spacing (grid 95%, off-grid only inside Pill), too many colours/fonts (3/6/3).

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)
1. Fix the keepsake voice in `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts`: title = the date, or the first clause of entry 1 verbatim (≤5 words, no comma-joins); only prepend a bridge when entry 2 doesn't already open with but/and/so, and strip that conjunction; keep the writer's own "i"; give each mood ≥4 closers or drop the closer entirely. Every timeline and memory screen improves at once.
2. onboarding-welcome: put the CTA + sign-in link in a StepShell-style footer so "hey pip" lands at the same y (~750) as "continue" on the next screen; the 198px void disappears and the run of five screens becomes one gesture.
3. Timeline header: drop "your story" from 32px to 24px Fredoka and fold "a whole week, kept." into the subtitle line, so the first 24px Fraunces date is the only large thing above the fold. Then unify BackLink labels to one pattern (destination name, no "keepsake edition").

## Notes for peers
- Interaction Engineer: reduced-motion kills opacity fades and Pip's breath outright (globals.css `animation: none` on `.animate-fade-up`, `.animate-pip-breathe`); bubbles have no press state.
- Systems Designer: dark-mode mood pills reuse light pastels (Mixed = #FFF3D1 on #26231F card, timeline-dark) — dark is an inversion here, not a second design; check the Heavy pill rendering in timeline-dark.
- Brand Advocate: `PaywallCard` aria-label is "unlock pip+" — the brand book bans "unlock"; invisible, but a screen reader hears it. Pause screen label "4-4-4-4 Box · switch rhythm" is jargon on the calm screen.
- Ergonomics: the timeline is only reachable via an unlabelled icon; verify whether that counts as discoverable for a tired user.
