# Pip UI council — round 06

## State of the UI

Round 6 is the most structurally disciplined build the council has seen and the first one where the failures are all *product* failures rather than craft failures. Every Tier A gate passes with room: 0 axe violations, 0 contrast failures, focus visible on 80/80 tabbed controls, 0/124 targets under 44px, 94.6% of spacing on the 8pt grid, exactly 6 type sizes / 3 families / 3 weights, 0 console errors, FCP ≤ 108ms, CLS ≤ 0.013. The type ramp is not merely compliant, it is structurally locked — `globals.css:150` wipes Tailwind's `--text-*` namespace so an unlisted size emits no CSS at all. The copy is genuinely unrepeatable: "a whole week, kept.", "there are 4 more days of you past the last week", "i'm a small app, not a person who can keep you safe right now — but there are people who can", "cancel anytime · export and delete stay free, always". What holds it back is that the two moments the product is actually sold on do not deliver. The keepsake — the artifact the $4.99 buys — hands the user their own two sentences back, recapitalised, and then reprints both of them 200px lower under "WHAT I SAID, THAT DAY"; on `memory-card-light-full.jpg` every word on the screen except one canned closing line is text the user typed. And the pause screen, whose entire job is to lower a heart rate, makes the honey exit button its loudest object, sets its phase heading in the memory serif, and — for a screen-reader user — announces the breathing phase roughly every four seconds for the whole session. The system underneath is excellent; the two payoffs sitting on top of it are hollow.

(Round 5 measured 471px here — narrowed, not fixed.)
2. **`settings-light` / `settings-dark` — untouched framework defaults left standing.** `globals.css` wipes the type namespace (`--text-*: initial`) but never the radius namespace, so Tailwind's defaults ship alongside the declared ladder: `rounded-2xl` ×17, `rounded-3xl` ×2, `rounded-xl` ×2, `rounded-t` ×1, against `rounded-card`/`rounded-pill`/`rounded-field` — and `rounded-field` is used once in the entire app. Visible on screen as a 20px HelpNowCard with a 40px chip sitting directly above 16px SettingRows with 36px chips.
