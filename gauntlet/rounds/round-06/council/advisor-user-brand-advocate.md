## Red flags

- **HIT — wrong balance / floaty spacing.** `onboarding-name-light`: the name field bottom sits at y≈358 and the `continue` button top at y≈790 — roughly 430px of untouched cream, over half the 844px screen, with nothing in it. Same shape on `onboarding-welcome-light` (~335px between the disclaimer and the `hey pip` button) and `onboarding-notify-light` (~350px). `StepShell` justifies it (`justify-start` so the answer doesn't jump when the keyboard opens) and the reason is sound, but on the two screens with no keyboard — welcome and notify — the page reads as a drifting island. Gate corroborates the same shape elsewhere: `meditation-pause` 144px void below the last control, both themes.
- **HIT — placeholder shipped as content.** `tests/e2e/fixtures/photo.jpg` is a 400×300 solid #F9C97C JPEG, seeded by `app/api/dev/seed/route.ts` onto the one day that carries a photo. It renders as a flat orange block in six screens: thread-light/dark (full-bleed, y≈88–245, the first thing on the home screen), thread-composing-photo-light/dark, timeline-light/dark-full (inside the "yesterday" keepsake), memory-card-light/dark-full (twice per page, ~310×230 and ~260×205). Not a code fault, but it is what rendered, and it is the difference between a keepsake and a broken image.
- **Checked and cleared — default/generic gradients.** The only gradient in the app is the radial sunlight→honey glow behind Pip (`PipMascot`), visible in onboarding-welcome-dark and meditation-pause-dark. That is the single permitted gradient in brand §6. No purple-blue anywhere.
- **Checked and cleared — glassmorphism/blur.** One blur, on `memory-locked-paywall-light/dark`, over "Sunday, August 30, 2026". It is motivated (this is the locked content) and it is not decorative frosting on chrome. Passes.
- **Checked and cleared — generic copy.** Read every h1, button label, chip, empty state, section header and system string. No "everything you need", "built for", "powerful", "unlock", "journey", "wellness". Closest to a platitude is "you're not alone" (help-light) and in a crisis context that is standard, not slop.
- **Checked and cleared — too many colours or fonts.** 3 families, 6 sizes, 3 weights, dominant neutral 62–96% on every screen. The support accents appear exactly where brand §6 puts them: `sky` on the calm mood dot and the `want to slow down for a sec?` chip, `blush` on the tender dot and the `get help now` card.
- **Checked and cleared — repeated card grids as filler.** The `EARLIER` run in timeline-full is five identical `CompactCard`s (Sun 6 → Wed 2), same date/pill/title/body rhythm. It is a list of days, and the two most recent days are deliberately given the fuller `MemoryCard` treatment above it — a hierarchy, not a grid of sameness.
- **Checked and cleared — default framework spacing.** Gate: 94.6% on 8pt; the off-grid residue is 4px×149 (legitimate micro-steps inside components) and 12px×4.

**Red-flag count: 2.**

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Make the keepsake stop repeating me.** On `memory-card-light-full` the reflection body ("Big review at work today, I was so nervous going in.") is verbatim my message, capitalised, and it sits ~200px above the identical raw line under WHAT I SAID, THAT DAY. Either the synthesis has to *say something I didn't say* — design-spec §4.4 asks for 3–5 sentences drawn from the day, and `scripted.ts` currently produces body = my sentences + one of six canned closings — or the day-detail page must collapse the source entries behind a "see what I actually wrote" disclosure so the two never share a screen. This is the moment the $4.99 has to justify itself and right now it visibly doesn't. And replace the fixture: any real photograph turns six screens from "broken" to "keepsake" for free.
2. **Give the pause screen back to the breath.** Demote `i feel ready` from the honey primary (320×56) to a ghost/text control, drop `PatternToggle` behind a small "switch pattern" affordance, and let the 240px ring + cozy Pip be the only loud thing on screen. Then close the 144px void by centring the whole stack. Right now the brightest, biggest object on the calm screen is the button that leaves it. Also move `state.phaseName` off `font-reading` back to Fredoka — the serif is the timeline's signal and spending it here costs the memory surfaces their meaning.
3. **Put the timeline in the thumb zone.** `MessageCircle` / `Settings` at y≈52 in the top bar means the payoff screen — the thing I'd pay for — is the hardest thing to reach one-handed at 1am, and design-spec §2 specifies a two-item bottom nav (Thread, Timeline) with settings on an avatar. Either ship that nav above the composer's safe area, or add a persistent low-right entry to "your story". Bonus: the `just paid? tap here` ghost button on `settings-subscription` should be a quiet auto-retry with a toast, not a visible support seam in the paid flow.

## Notes for peers

- **Seven days, one person?** Mostly yes — the titles genuinely differ in register ("Three days of fog just lifted" / "Felt genuinely peaceful for once" / "We're getting dinner next week" / "Small win but I'll take it" / "I got through it, and that counts"), and `scripted.ts` does real work to avoid sameness: six closings per mood, a seed derived from the day's own text, and an echo check that steps past a closing repeating a word the body just used. **But one day breaks continuity and a human would catch it.** Sun Sep 6 is titled "Three days of fog just lifted" and tagged `mixed`, while its own body ("Went for a walk under the amber leaves after lunch. I stopped trying to name it.") mentions no fog — and the three days *before* it in the same list are calm, tender and bright. The mood misfires because `moodFrom()` matches none of that day's words. Interaction Engineer / Systems seat: worth checking whether the title should be excluded from the body only when the mood classifier actually understood it.
- **Settings state contradicts onboarding.** `settings-light`/`dark` shows "your rhythm & nudges — check-ins off" in the same fixture where `onboarding-rhythm` set 08:30 AM / 09:00 PM. Ergonomics seat may want to confirm whether that's a seed artefact or a real write-path gap.
- **Two good lines are below the 844px fold and I could not score them.** `CRISIS_CARD_FOOTER` — "if i misread this, no worries at all — i'd always rather check than not." — is the line that stops a false positive feeling like an accusation, and it sits under the two 988 buttons, off-screen in `thread-crisis-light`. Likewise the `SystemMoment` "your day is ready to look back on" reveal appears in none of the 23 renders. If the next round adds screens, those two are worth more than another settings shot.
- Taste Critic: the `A3.void` advisory numbers understate the onboarding case, because those screens are marked "scrolls" rather than measured. Please eyeball `onboarding-name-light` yourself.
