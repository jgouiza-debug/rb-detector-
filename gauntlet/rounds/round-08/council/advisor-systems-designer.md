## Red flags

- **`DangerZone.tsx:36` — `font-mono` on the word "delete" — "too many fonts."** Brand §7 says "Two families, not three." `--font-mono` was left in the Tailwind namespace while `--text-*` was wiped, and this line spends it. One word on one screen, and defensible as a type-to-confirm string, but it is a third family shipping in the build and the machine never saw it.

Everything else on the checklist, checked and clear:
- **Default/generic gradients — none found.** I grepped every `gradient` occurrence in `app/` and `components/`: five are `mask-image` on `.scroll-fade-x`, one is the paywall scrim (`MemoryDetail.tsx:63`, transparent→`bg`), and `PipMascot.tsx:38-46` is the sanctioned `#FFDE7A`→`#F5B841` radial glow behind Pip. No purple, no unmotivated hero gradient.
- **Default framework spacing — none found.** Steps used are 0/1/2/3/4/6/8/10 only; zero `p-5`/`gap-7`/`py-9`/`mt-11`; 12px appears twice, both intentional negative offsets.
- **Colours mushed together — none found.** 23/23 screens pass the 70/20/10 proxy; the neutral family is 52.6–96.4% of every viewport and the two accents never both exceed 20% except on `thread-crisis` where the pip-bubble surface is the screen.
- **Card-grid filler — none found.** The "earlier" run of five `CompactCard`s is uniform, but today/yesterday get the full 24px-title card with photos and older days get an 18px compact card — a deliberate two-tier treatment, the opposite of filler.
- **Generic copy — none found.** Checked every visible string on all 28 renders plus the subscription feature list and mood filters. No SaaS boilerplate.
- **Blur/glass because possible — none found.** The single `blur-[3px]` is load-bearing: it is the locked memory.
- **Wrong balance — cleared after measurement.** The 181px band on `settings-subscription-light` and 136px on `help-light` are the anchored-CTA pattern the taste brief mandates, not floaty spacing; `meditation-pause` (144px above / 156px below) and `onboarding-notify` (192/187) are symmetric centring. I flagged none of these.

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Seal the radius ladder the way the type ramp is sealed.** Add `--radius-*: initial;` beside `--text-*: initial;` in the `@theme` block, then replace the 21 off-ladder uses: `rounded-2xl`→`rounded-field` on every input and `SettingRow` (making `--radius-field` earn its declaration), `rounded-3xl`→`rounded-bubble` on `Composer.tsx:125` so the composer stops out-rounding the bubbles above it, `rounded-xl`→`rounded-card` on `Skeleton.tsx:4`, and `rounded-card` on `HelpNowCard.tsx:7`→`rounded-field` so the settings list is one radius top to bottom. That takes six rendered non-pill radii down to three and makes drift impossible for the next round.
2. **Give the onboarding CTA a fixed line.** The primary button's centre moves 68px between step 4 (y=646) and step 5 (y=578). Change `StepShell.tsx:23` from `flex-1 justify-center` on the whole group to a centred content block plus `mt-auto` on the footer, so every step's button lands at the same y — the button then reads as one object the user presses five times, not five buttons in five places.
3. **Make the disclosure look tappable and give the mood pill one grammar.** Add a rotating `ChevronDown` to `MemoryDetail.tsx:124`'s summary (`list-none` currently leaves zero affordance on "WHAT I ACTUALLY WROTE (2)"), and pass `moodIcon[day.mood]` into `CompactCard.tsx:75`'s `Pill` so the same token renders the same way in the two card tiers that sit 200px apart on `timeline-light-full`. While there, drop `moodLabel` in favour of `mood.label` at `MemoryDetail.tsx:93` so the pill stops saying "Bright & warm" in Title Case in an all-lowercase app.
