# Taste Critic — round 08

Could this only be Pip? Mostly, yes — and that is a real achievement. The voice is one voice: "keep it all · $4.99/mo", "not right now", "there are 4 more days of you past the last week", "2 moments kept", "kept by pip", "noted quietly · no reply". Newsreader carries every heading and the whole keepsake; Inter stays out of the way; the palette is honestly cream/honey/ink with pastels only on mood. Nothing here is purple. What keeps this out of the 90s is the product's one sacred artifact: the keepsake is a template, and on `timeline-light-full.jpg` you can watch it being a template.

## Red flags
- `memory-locked-paywall-light.jpg` / `-dark.jpg`: locked card — `blur-[3px] saturate-50` over a **skeleton** (five `bg-surface-2` bars, no words), plus a `bg-gradient-to-b from-transparent to-bg` fade. Blurring placeholder bars is theatre: there is nothing behind the frost to protect. The real date is the only real thing, and it would be more honest un-blurred. (blur because it was possible)
- `settings-subscription-light.jpg`: ~190px of empty cream between "export and delete stay free, always" (y≈550) and the sticky CTA (y≈740). (floaty spacing / no balance)
- `timeline-light-full.jpg`: the seven "earlier"/card closings — one metre, one length, one full stop each, drawn from a fixed pool. Not generic in the SaaS sense, but templated in the AI sense, and visible as such when seven sit in a column. (copy smell)
- None found; checked: gradients (only Pip's radial `#FFE8A0→#FFDE7A→#F5B841`, the permitted sunlight→honey glow, and the locked-page fade above); default framework spacing (`px-4 py-2` is the universal recipe but everything is on 8/16/24 and `--text-*: initial` wipes the default ramp); colour/font count (2 families, palette disciplined, 70/20/10 23/23); card-grid filler (the "earlier" list is content; the subscription check list is 5 rows, one column); banned words ("unlock/powerful/journey" absent from visible copy — the one "unlock" is `aria-label="unlock pip+"` on PaywallCard.tsx, heard by screen readers only; fix it anyway); animation (every animation has a job; idle Pip, arrivals, pacer).

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)
1. **Make the keepsake write about the day, not hand the day back.** In scripted.ts, `body` is the user's first line verbatim and `closing` is a pool pick. Even offline, compose: fold the last line into a sentence ("I got through it — home now, and that counts."), vary closing *length* (one clause, one full sentence, or none), and let some days end on the user's own words with no coda. Kill "It got under my ribs a bit." The pool currently exists to avoid repetition; the tell is cadence, not repetition.
2. **Un-blur the paywall.** Show the real date at 32px Newsreader in `fg-soft`, no skeleton bars, no `blur`; move the PaywallCard up so the two objects don't have equal mass. On subscription, drop `mt-auto` on the CTA wrapper (or add the plan card's renewal/"what changes tonight" line) so the list and the button are one object.
3. **One radius scale, one label case.** Collapse `rounded-2xl` (17 uses, settings rows + inputs) into `--radius-field`/`--radius-card`, and make the composer textarea `rounded-bubble`. Lowercase `moodLabel` ("bright & warm") so the keepsake speaks in the same voice as the pill under it on the timeline.
