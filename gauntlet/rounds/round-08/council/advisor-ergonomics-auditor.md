## Red flags
- **Vibe-coded checklist (RUBRIC.md list):** none found. Checked: gradients (only the brand-approved sunlight glow keyframe in `globals.css`), Tailwind-default spacing (found one real instance, see A3, but it's a single 12px slip not a pattern), color/font count (6/2/3, disciplined), repeated card grids (timeline's day cards are genuine repeating data, not filler), generic SaaS copy (none — copy is Pip-specific throughout), glassmorphism/blur-for-its-own-sake (the paywall's `blur-[3px] saturate-50` on the locked keepsake is a deliberate, commented design choice showing the day's *shape* without its words, not decoration), type/spacing balance (onboarding and thread both read calm, not cramped or floaty).
- `thread-light.jpg` / `components/chat/MessageList.tsx`: `role="log"` region starts empty on mount and is filled with up to 60 history messages by a post-mount `useEffect` in `Thread.tsx` — a real risk of a screen reader reading the entire scrollback aloud on every visit to the home screen, not caught by the gate's static-DOM audit.
- `components/pip/PipMascot.tsx`: SVG gradient `id`s are keyed only by `expression` (`pip-listening-body`), so any screen with two or more same-expression `PipAvatar`s (e.g. `thread-light.jpg`) ships duplicate DOM ids.
- `app/goodbye/page.tsx`: zero interactive elements, no way back into the app — a genuine dead end outside the gate's 23-screen set.

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)
1. Pre-hydrate the thread store from the server-rendered `initial` prop (e.g. `useState(() => initial)` or a Zustand-provider seeded per request) instead of an empty store + post-mount `setMessages`, so a screen reader never encounters a `role="log"` that jumps from 0 to 60 items in one tick.
2. Namespace `PipMascot`'s gradient ids per instance (`useId()` or a passed `instanceId`) so repeated avatars on one screen don't collide.
3. Give `app/goodbye/page.tsx` at least one way forward (a "start again" or "close" affordance) — right now it's a dead end the gate's fixture never visits.
