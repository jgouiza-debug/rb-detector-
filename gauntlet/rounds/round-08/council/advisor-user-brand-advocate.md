**Does the keepsake read as something written *about* my day, or as my own sentences handed back?** Mostly the second, and that is the round's real achievement. `synthesizeDay` (scripted.ts) takes my last message of the day as the title and everything before it as the body: "but i made it through, and i'm home now" becomes the headline "I made it through, and I'm home now", and "today was a lot honestly. work was heavy and i didn't stop once" becomes the opening line. It is *mine*. `<details>` even offers me the receipt — "what i actually wrote (2)". Two things stop it short of perfect. First, `sentence()` capitalises my `i` into `I` and normalises my run-ons; that's defensible for a keepsake, but it is an edit of me, and it strips the hedge — "but i made it through" loses its "but". Second, the last sentence is always borrowed. Across the seven visible days I can already see the seam: "Not every day has to be more than survived." (Sep 8), "It asked a lot. I paid it." (Sep 2), "I stopped trying to name it." (Sep 6). The anti-echo and per-day seeding are genuinely careful work, and none repeats — but nobody types like that, and after a month I will notice that every card ends in a voice that isn't mine. That is the §11 "fake-deep" line, in the one place the product cannot afford it.

## Red flags

- **settings-subscription-light: the checklist → CTA gap — wrong balance / floaty spacing.** ~180px of bare cream between "export and delete stay free, always" and "keep it all · $4.99/mo" (`mt-auto` + `sticky bottom-0`), on a screen the gate itself records at 81% 8pt-conformance. The list ends, then nothing, then the ask. Give the list a heading ("what pip+ adds") and let the block sit up.
- Default / generic gradients — **none found.** Checked every gradient in the tree: the Pip body radial (#FFE8A0→#FFDE7A, the sanctioned sunlight glow), `--animate-glow`, and one `bg-gradient-to-b from-transparent to-bg` scrim over the locked day. No purple-blue, no unmotivated hero wash.
- Default framework spacing — **none found.** `--text-*: initial` wipes Tailwind's ramp so an unlisted size produces no CSS; 12px appears 4 times app-wide.
- Too many colours or fonts — **none found.** 2 families, 3 weights. The five mood hues (sunlight/sky/blush/sage/heavy-tint) all land in one 32px-dot row in `WeekFlow`; that is How-We-Feel-style colour-as-feeling, not a status bar, and the 70/20/10 proxy holds (timeline-light: surface 51.9% + cream 39.8%).
- Repeated card grids as filler — **none found.** The five stacked `CompactCard`s under "EARLIER" are content, not padding, and the today/yesterday cards use a visibly richer shape.
- Generic copy — **none found.** No "unlock", no "journey", no "powerful", no "everything you need". Checked every CTA, heading and empty state; "your first keepsake arrives tonight." is as close to a stock line as it gets, and it isn't one.
- Glassmorphism / gratuitous blur — **none found.** The only blur is `blur-[3px] saturate-50` on the locked day, which exists to show shape without words. Motivated.

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Stop borrowing the last sentence of my keepsake.** Drop `CLOSINGS` entirely and let the reflection end on my own final clause, or reserve the pooled line for days with a single entry where there is nothing else to close on. If a closing must stay, cut the pool down to plain observations ("that was the day.") and delete the aphorisms — "It asked a lot. I paid it." is the one place in the whole build where I can hear a machine trying to be profound.
2. **Give "what i actually wrote (2)" an affordance, and stage the reveal.** Add a chevron that rotates on open (`details[open] summary svg { rotate: 90deg }`) so the receipt is discoverable; and make today's keepsake arrive rather than exist — from the `day_ready` pill in the thread, push to `/memory/[date]` with the card entering alone on `animate-keepsake-in` before the timeline list is offered. Right now the tiny gift is a list item.
3. **Fix the two places the tone slips.** (a) settings-subscription: add "what pip+ adds" above the list and drop the ✓ circles to plain dots while on free — checkmarks on features I don't have is the one dark-pattern-shaped thing in an otherwise honest paywall. (b) Make `moodLabel` lowercase ("bright & warm") so it matches the pill on the timeline and the rest of the product's voice, and pass the real `lockedCount` into `PaywallCard` from `MemoryDetail` so the locked day gets "there are 4 more days of you" too.

## Notes for peers

- **Systems Designer:** `--font-display` and `--font-reading` are the same `var(--font-serif)` (globals.css:163–165). Any claim in the code that the pause screen speaks in a different face than the memory card is untrue in the pixels — worth confirming against your A4 read.
- **Taste Critic:** the `CLOSINGS` pool in `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts` is the template smell I could find in an otherwise template-free build; it is invisible in a single screenshot and only shows up across the seven timeline cards in `timeline-light-full.jpg`.
- **Ergonomics auditor:** meditation-pause presents three controls where the primary is the softest variant, and thread-crisis puts a five-region resource table above the 988 buttons. Both pass the machine gates; both cost decisions at the worst possible moment.
- **Interaction Engineer:** please verify `MemoryDetail`'s `<details>` — `list-none` with no replacement marker means the disclosure may be invisible as an affordance on every platform, not just in the screenshot.
