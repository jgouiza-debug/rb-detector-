## Red flags

Checklist walked item by item.

- **Default or generic gradients — none found.** I checked every gradient in the build: two `radialGradient`s inside `PipMascot.tsx` (body shading, and the sunlight→honey glow the brand explicitly permits) and one `bg-gradient-to-b from-surface to-bg` on the pause screen (`#FFFFFF`→`#FFF9ED` light, `#26231F`→`#1C1A17` dark) — a same-family warm wash motivated by "dim the surroundings". No purple-blue, no multi-hue, nothing unmotivated.
- **Default framework spacing — HIT.** settings-light: the "get help now" card (`rounded-card`, 20px) and the "account" row (`rounded-2xl`, 16px) sit 22px apart at the same width with the same px-4/py-3 padding and different corner radii. The project's own `--radius-field` token is used once while its Tailwind-default neighbour `rounded-2xl` is used 21 times; `gap-3`/`px-3`/`py-3` (12px) appears 46 times and `gap-5`/`px-5`/`p-5` (20px) 14 times — the two values the taste brief names as the tell.
- **Too many colours or fonts / no 70/20/10 — none found.** Three families, three weights, every on-screen hex traceable to `tokens.ts` bar two raw dark surfaces, and the gate's pixel census substantiates the ratio (neutral ≥84% on 19 of 21 screens, honey ≈5%).
- **Repeated card/badge grids as filler — none found.** Checked timeline-light-full and timeline-dark-full end to end: the timeline deliberately tiers its cards (expanded for today/yesterday, compact for the five "EARLIER" days) rather than stacking one shape, and every card holds real content.
- **Generic copy — HIT.** settings-subscription-light: "upgrade to Pip+ ($4.99/mo)" and "refresh status". Title-cased product name, parenthesised price, a growth-team verb and an engineering noun, on the one screen the round's lowercase-and-warmth pass missed. (No "everything you need", "built for", "powerful", "journey" or "unlock" survives in the pixels — I checked all 26.)
- **Glassmorphism / blur / animation because it's possible — none found.** `backdrop-blur-md` appears exactly twice, on `TopBar` and `Composer`, both sticky bars with content scrolling underneath — where blur earns its keep. `Sheet` uses a plain `backdrop:bg-ink/40` scrim, no blur. No frosted cards anywhere.
- **Wrong balance / floaty spacing — HIT.** memory-card-light and -dark: content ends at y≈530 of an 844px page (the `-full` files are byte-identical to the viewport shots), leaving 37% empty with nothing anchored. settings-subscription-light: ~500px between a 96px card and the buttons. memory-locked-paywall-light/dark: ~415px of flat, undimmed field above the sheet with a lone back-link floating in it — a page composed as a sheet without anything behind it. onboarding-welcome-light/dark: CTA at y≈566 with ~220px dead below, while the other four onboarding steps anchor at y≈792–840.

**Red-flag count: 3** (default framework spacing; generic copy; wrong balance/floaty spacing). 4 checked clean.

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Collapse the app onto the ladder it already declares.** `globals.css` defines field/bubble/card/pill; the render uses seven radii. Replace all 21 `rounded-2xl` with `rounded-field` or `rounded-card` by role, and the composer's `rounded-3xl` with the same field token, so the input role has one shape across onboarding-name, thread-light and timeline-light instead of three (16 / 24 / 999). In the same pass drop `p-7` from `PaywallCard` to `p-5` so the timeline stack reads 16/20/20 rather than 16/20/28, and settle the six mood glyphs on one size instead of 13px in the Pill, 15px in the WeekFlow dot and 15px in the chips. Seven radii and ten icon sizes become four and three, and nothing on screen has to change shape more than a pixel or two.
2. **Give the payoff screens a bottom.** memory-card-light ends at y≈530 of 844: put the day's photos on the card (design-spec §3.3 asks for them and no photo appears in any of the 26 shots) and anchor a previous/next-day control in the thumb zone. Do the same on settings-subscription-light. And route the welcome screen through `StepShell` so its CTA anchors at y≈792 like the other four onboarding steps instead of floating at y≈566 — the shell already handles the absent progress bar, so this is a deletion, not a feature.
3. **Fix the reflection generator, because it is the product.** Kill the "Later," joint — it appears in all seven reflections on timeline-light-full and produces non-sentences ("Later, but i made it through, and i'm home now"). Stop titling compact cards with the opening words of their own preview, which makes every card say the same phrase twice and yields titles that end mid-clause ("Hard day honestly, everything"). Normalise capitalisation inside a reflection ("Today was a lot honestly. work was heavy…"). Fix "i'll take it…." — ellipsis plus period. And move "breathe in…" off Fraunces (`BreathingPacer.tsx:69`) so the serif still means "this is a memory".

## Notes for peers

**The worktree code is not the code that produced these screenshots.** Verified by string search: "the days you gave pip, kept.", "last 7 days", "search your days…", "all days", "2 moments kept", "keep it all · $4.99/mo", "not right now" and "you're not alone" are all visible in the round-01 pixels and exist **nowhere** in `app/`, `components/` or `lib/`. The worktree still says "Your Story", "All Memories", "Search memories, words, or feelings…", "7-day window", "read thread", "pip captured 2 moments", and `PaywallCard.tsx` still ships a CTA reading "Unlock with Pip+ ($4.99/mo)" — the exact word the taste brief bans, which the render has already replaced with "keep it all". **Do not score the builder on those strings; the pixels prove they were fixed.** Some files *are* current and safe to cite: the gate report's own axe node quotes `placeholder="tell pip anything…" aria-label="message pip"`, matching `Composer.tsx` exactly, and `WeekFlow.tsx`, `SubscriptionActions.tsx`, `HelpNowCard.tsx` and `moodIcons.ts` match their screenshots string-for-string. I anchored every score to a screenshot or a gate number and used code only as corroboration.

**The gate's A3 and A4 censuses are narrower than they read.** A3 reports zero values off the 4pt half-grid, but `Bubble.tsx:24` sets every chat bubble to `py-2.5` (10px) and `MessageList.tsx:25` gaps them at `gap-1.5` (6px) — the chat's entire rhythm. A4 reports six type sizes, but the source carries `text-[15px]`, `text-[11px]` and `text-[17px]`, and in timeline-light the WeekFlow weekday letters and the numerals beneath the dots are visibly two different sizes. Whoever cross-checks Tier A should decide whether those censuses need widening before round 02 leans on them.

**For the interaction engineer:** I could not verify stagger or the typing indicator from stills. `--animate-bubble-in` is 380ms on `cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1)` — above the 150–300ms band and with real overshoot on every message arrival.

**For the accessibility auditor:** the gate lists a moderate `region` violation on thread/light, thread/dark and thread-crisis/light — the composer textarea outside any landmark on the home screen. Below the serious+ threshold so the gate passes, but it is the one A1 residual I found, and it is on the screen users spend all their time in.

**For the taste critic:** the "Later," template and the duplicated compact-card titles are the strongest slop signal in the build, and they sit on the payoff screen. Also worth your eye: `Contrast` as the "mixed" mood glyph is the only UI-chrome icon in an otherwise organic set, and it is what tips the WeekFlow strip toward a status bar.
