## Red flags
- timeline-light-full: EARLIER compact cards ×5 — **repeated shapes as filler**. Every card's Fraunces title is the first four words of its own body ("Went for a walk / Went for a walk under the amber leaves…", "Hard day honestly, everything / Hard day honestly, everything felt heavy…"); each body splices with "Later, …". Five identical cards saying each thing twice is machine filler on the payoff screen.
- timeline-light + memory-card-light: reflection copy — **generic/robotic copy**. "Today was a lot honestly. work was heavy and i didn't stop once. Later, but i made it through, and i'm home now." Sentence case and lowercase-i collide inside one sentence; "Later, but" is not a sentence a person writes. This is `scripted.ts`-shaped output rendered as the keepsake.
- settings-subscription-light: "refresh status" button + "upgrade to Pip+ ($4.99/mo)" — **generic copy**. Utility-panel language in a product whose paywall says "keep it all". Also **floaty**: ≈500px of cream between the plan card (bottom y≈214) and the CTA (y≈732).
- memory-locked-paywall-light/dark: **wrong balance**. Back link at y≈45, then nothing until the card at y≈460. Half a phone of empty ground before the ask.
- TopBar/Composer (`bg-bg/90 backdrop-blur-md`, `bg-bg/95 backdrop-blur-md`): **blur because it's possible** — behind a 90–95% opaque bar the blur is imperceptible; it costs a compositor layer and buys nothing.
- meditation-pause: "4-4-4-4 Box · switch rhythm" pill — **copy that could be any breathing app**; a calm screen should not name its algorithm.
- Checked and clean: gradients (only the PipMascot radial `#FFE8A0→#FFDE7A→#F5B841` and the cozy glow — both permitted); fonts/weights (3/3); palette discipline on every non-timeline screen (cream ≥ 84% dominant); default Tailwind spacing (card insets render 24px, rows 16px — chosen, not left standing).

**Red-flag count: 6.**

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)
1. **Make the keepsake worth keeping.** Kill the "title = first four words" rule and the "Later, …" splice; one case, one voice. Date on one line (drop the year on the card, or 28px). Below the reflection, show that day's photos or its two entries inline instead of ≈320px of cream. This lifts B5, C2, C3 together.
2. **Quiet the timeline top.** Drop the "this week's flow" card frame and set the seven dots directly under "your story" as the header's second line; move search behind the magnifier in the top bar; move mood filters into the search sheet. Target: first keepsake above y≈300, cream as the single ground.
3. **One voice for money.** Reuse the paywall card verbatim on settings/subscription (cozy Pip, "keep it all · $4.99/mo", "not right now"), delete "refresh status" (sync on focus), and on memory-locked-paywall place the card directly under the back link instead of pinned to the bottom.
