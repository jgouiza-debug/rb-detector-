## Red flags

1. **`timeline-light-full` — whole page: identical section shapes stacked down the page.** The WeekFlow, the search field, both large memory cards, all five compact cards and the paywall render as the same 358px-wide `rounded-card bg-surface` rectangle in one column. Ten stacked rounded rectangles, one texture, no rhythm.
2. **`timeline-light-full` / `memory-card-light` — reflection text: generic, formulaic copy.** "Later," is the connective in 7/7 reflections; the closers are per-mood boilerplate ("It was heavy, and I'm still here. That counts." / "A bit of everything today, and I'm okay with that."); titles are the first four words verbatim, producing "Hard day honestly, everything".
3. **`settings-subscription-light` — CTA and secondary button: copy that could belong to any app.** "upgrade to Pip+ ($4.99/mo)" and "refresh status". "refresh status" is engineering language on a user's screen, and "upgrade to … ($…)" is the growth register the paywall screen deliberately avoids.
4. **Wrong balance — floaty voids on three screens.** `settings-subscription-light`: a 96px card at the top, then ~500px of empty cream before the buttons. `memory-card-light`: card ends ~y530, ~310px of nothing under it. `onboarding-name-light`: field at ~y490, "continue" at ~y816, ~300px gap with nothing in it.
5. **`thread-light` / `thread-dark` — composer bar: blur because it's possible.** `backdrop-blur-md` sits behind `bg-bg/95`; a 95%-opaque surface has nothing to blur through.

Checked and **not** found: default/generic gradients (the only gradients in the build are Pip's `#FFE8A0 → #FFDE7A → #F5B841` radial body and its glow — the one gradient the brand permits; no purple-blue anywhere). Too many colours or fonts (3 families, 3 weights, 6 sizes; neutral family ≥85% of pixels on 21/21 screens). Untouched Tailwind default spacing (95.2% on 8pt, 0 off the 4pt half-grid). Glassmorphism as an aesthetic (only the one blur above; no frosted panels).

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Rewrite the keepsake so it isn't visibly stitched.** Kill the "Later," bridge and the fixed per-mood closers in `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts:60`, and stop deriving titles from `slice(0,4)` words — "Hard day honestly, everything" is a broken sentence on the one screen that has to feel precious. If a fixture must be deterministic, hand-write seven distinct reflections in first person with different shapes (one two-sentence, one that ends on an image, one that just names the feeling). This single change moves C3, B5 and my willingness to pay more than any pixel work on this list.
2. **Make the paywall show me the day it's withholding, and make the reveal a gift.** `memory-locked-paywall-light` should render the locked memory card behind the sheet — date legible, reflection softly veiled — so "4 more days of you" has a face. And change `SystemMoment.tsx`'s day_ready pill from `bg-surface-2` to a warm pip-bubble surface with a small Pip beside it; a grey chip is not a tiny gift.
3. **Put Pip back in the thread and fix dark mode's warmth polarity.** Seed Pip replies into the history (`app/api/dev/seed/route.ts` currently writes user-only rows, so Sat Sep 5 and Sun Sep 6 show four of my bubbles and zero from Pip). Then in dark mode, drop the user bubble from #FFCF4D to a deeper honey so 26.2% of a near-black screen stops glaring at 1am, and give Pip's bubble a warm tint instead of neutral grey — right now the dark theme makes me the warm one and Pip the cold one.

## Notes for peers

- **Systems Designer:** the back-link labels are a system break, not a copy nit — "back to pip", "settings", "your story", "back", "keepsake edition" across five screens, and the last one names the page you're already on. Also confirm whether the two card grammars on the timeline (full card for today/yesterday, compact card for "EARLIER") are one component with a variant or two one-offs.
- **Interaction Engineer:** `components/timeline/MemoryCard.tsx`'s `<Link>` and the `ThreadTopBar` icon links carry `hover:` only, no `active:`. Please verify whether anything gives touch feedback on the timeline at all. Also check whether the `backdrop-blur-md` under `bg-bg/95` costs a compositor layer for zero visual benefit.
- **Taste Critic:** the "Later," tell is worth your own look — it is the clearest AI-assembly seam in the build and it is on the payoff screen. Second opinion wanted on "4-4-4-4 Box · switch rhythm" (`meditation-pause-light`): I read it as borrowed breathwork-app language on Pip's calmest surface.
- **Ergonomics Auditor:** the mood filter chip row on `timeline-light` is cut mid-chip at the viewport edge with no gradient or scroll indicator — the gate reports no page overflow, but a thumb has no way to know there's more.
- **Everyone:** the Fraunces reading serif is used on `meditation-pause-light` ("breathe in…"). The taste brief reserves it for memory surfaces "so the shift means something". Worth a ruling on whether the pause screen counts as a slow surface or dilutes the reservation.
