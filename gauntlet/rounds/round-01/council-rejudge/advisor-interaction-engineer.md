## Red flags
- `settings-subscription-light.jpg` (and dark) — the page body between the "current plan / free" card and the "upgrade to Pip+" CTA stack is ~420px of unbroken `--bg` with zero content (`app/(app)/settings/subscription/page.tsx`). That's the rubric's "wrong balance: cramped or floaty spacing, no breathing room" flag — this isn't intentional whitespace, it's an empty screen.
- Checked and NOT flagging as a red flag, but noting for peers: `Composer.tsx:70` uses `bg-bg/95 backdrop-blur-md` on the sticky footer. This is functional (legibility of the input bar over scrolling messages), not decorative glassmorphism, so I did not count it — but the Taste Critic should eyeball it since blur is explicitly named in the red-flag list.
- Checked for: default/generic gradients (none — only the brand-permitted sunlight→honey glow), untouched Tailwind spacing (none — the 8pt-grid gate is 95.2% and the 4px deltas are all component-internal micro-steps, not page-margin drift), too many colors/fonts (gate caps hit exactly: 6 sizes/3 families/3 weights), repeated card-grid filler (none — Timeline's day list is real content, not decorative filler), generic SaaS copy (none found in any of the 26 screens).

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)
1. Bring `--animate-bubble-in` (380ms) and `--animate-fade-up` (320ms) inside the 150–300ms band (`app/globals.css:161-162`). These two classes drive nearly every "something appeared" moment in the app (every chat bubble, every memory card, the toast, the crisis card, the system moment chip, the sheet) — a single CSS edit (e.g. 260ms / 220ms) fixes the timing on the whole product at once.
2. Give `components/settings/SettingRow.tsx` a press state (`active:scale-[0.98]` + `transition-transform duration-150`, matching `Button.tsx`) so all five settings rows confirm a tap before navigating. Do the same for the tab buttons in `MemoryDetail.tsx:75-76` (add `transition-colors duration-150`).
3. Fill or remove the dead space in `app/(app)/settings/subscription/page.tsx` between the plan card and the CTA — either move the CTA up under the plan card, or put something in that gap (what Pip+ unlocks, a comparison, anything). Also make the whole `MemoryCard` tappable (wrap the card in the day-detail `Link`, not just the trailing "read thread" text) to match the spec's "tap a day → day detail" and the pattern already used correctly in `SettingRow`.
