# Superseded

This council judged round 1 with a jury drawn entirely from one small model family
(the larger models were rate-limited mid-loop). It returned a median of **96.5** with
3 red flags, and its own chair rated the jury **low confidence**.

A mixed Opus/Fable/Sonnet jury re-judged the same screenshots and the same gate report
and returned **77**, with 4 red flags. The re-judge in [`../council-rejudge/`](../council-rejudge)
is the authoritative verdict for round 1; `gauntlet/scripts/orchestrate.ts` reads it
in preference to this directory.

Everything here is kept deliberately. It is the clearest evidence in the repo that a
single-family jury inflates — 19.5 points on identical artefacts — which is the exact
failure the gauntlet exists to prevent.
