# Council report — round 01

Chair synthesis of five advisor reviews, five peer reviews, the machine gate report, and my own spot-verification of ten screenshots (`timeline-light-full`, `timeline-dark`, `thread-light`, `thread-crisis-light`, `memory-card-light-full`, `memory-locked-paywall-light`, `meditation-pause-dark`, `settings-light`, `onboarding-welcome-light`, `help-light`). Builder notes, scoreboard and ledger not read. The round-00 council report was read for the regression check only.

## State of the UI

Round 01 is a real repair, not a re-skin. Every one of the five machine gates flipped from FAIL to PASS: the timeline no longer renders 644px wide inside a 390px phone, the paywall no longer logs a 402, the type ramp is back to the six sizes it declares, and 0 of 217 targets sit under 44px. The fixes reached the content layer too — I read all seven memory cards on `timeline-light-full` and no two share a closing sentence any more ("It was heavy, and I'm still here. That counts." / "A bit of everything today, and I'm okay with that."), Pip's replies in `thread-light` no longer repeat verbatim ("that sounds heavy." → "you don't have to sort it out right now. what's the part sitting with you most?" → "you made it through."), the banned verb is gone from the paywall ("keep your whole story" · "keep it all · $4.99/mo" · "not right now" · "cancel anytime · export and delete stay free, always"), and `meditation-pause` has lost its countdown numeral, its ticking media clock and its full-bleed grey gradient — it is now a single glowing Pip inside a thin amber ring under "breathe in… / slow, through your nose". What is left is composition, not correctness. `timeline` still fires seven objects before one word of the user's memory and still guillotines its last filter chip at the frame edge in both themes; `memory-locked-paywall` floats its card after more than half a screen of empty cream; the keepsake and settings pages end early over 240–310px of void. Those are the four points still on the table, and they are all layout.

## Red flags

Union of everything raised, each verified by me against the captures. Advisor-side, four of five reviews reported **zero** red flags; the Ergonomics Auditor raised six, of which three do not survive verification. Below is what does.

1. **`timeline` (light + dark): the mood-filter row** — wrong balance / cramped. The row's last chip is guillotined by the right frame edge, rendering as "ca" with no fade, mask, or scroll affordance to say it continues. Chair-verified identically in `timeline-light-full.jpg` and `timeline-dark.jpg`. Round 00 flagged this same row cut mid-chip as a symptom of the +254px overflow; the overflow is fixed, the clipped chip is not.
2. **`memory-locked-paywall` (light + dark), `memory-card` (both), `settings` (both): page composition** — wrong balance, floaty. On the paywall the back-link sits at y≈45 and then nothing happens until the card starts at y≈460 of an 844px viewport — over half the screen is bare cream, undimmed, with no preview of the locked memory behind it. The keepsake card ends at y≈530 with ~310px of void; settings ends at y≈600 with ~240px. Round 00 flagged this family on the same three screens; it is the least-changed thing in the round.
3. **`memory-locked-paywall` (both themes) + the `timeline` paywall block: body copy** — generic. "pip+ keeps every day for as long as you want it, **and lets pip go deeper with you**." Chair-verified verbatim on two surfaces. It is the one clause in the product that could sit inside any wellness app, and it sits on the only screen asking for money. Round 00's banned "Unlock with Pip+" is gone; this is what replaced it.

**Dropped after verification:**
- *`memory-card` tab styling diverges from the button system* (raised by Ergonomics, upheld by Interaction Engineer, and the sole reason for two B2 sixes) — refuted by chair spot-check: the ink-filled selected pill on `memory-card` is the same token as "all days" on `timeline`, in both themes. It is a consistent selection style, not a one-off.
- *`meditation-pause` dark: Pip glow ring may read under 3:1* — inspected in `meditation-pause-dark.jpg`; the amber ring and its progress dot are plainly legible on `night`, and the gate measures 0 contrast failures on that screen. Not a hit.
- *The glow animation may be running unscoped outside the pause screen* — no glow artefact appears on `thread`, `timeline`, `settings`, `help` or `memory-card` in either theme. The soft halo on `onboarding-welcome` is the brand's one permitted sunlight→honey glow behind Pip. Not a hit.
- *`region` landmark violations as a red flag* — real and logged by the machine, but it is an accessibility defect, not a vibe-coded pattern. Scored under A1 (11.5) instead.
- *Repeated card stack on `timeline` "EARLIER"* (round 00 flag #3) — the five compact cards still share one shape, but the repetition is now the content: five real, distinct days with distinct titles, moods and text. Same exclusion round 00 applied to the helpline directory. Not a hit this round.

**Final red-flag count: 3** (down from 8).

## Note on the jury
