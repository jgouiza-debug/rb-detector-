## Red flags

- **timeline-light-full / timeline-dark-full — the `earlier` section**: repeated card grid as filler. Six `CompactCard`s of identical shape, padding and internal rhythm stacked Sep 6 → Sep 2; only the mood pill distinguishes them. Give the run a shape change (a date rule every N days, or drop the pill for a left mood-edge) so the eye has somewhere to rest.
- **onboarding-welcome-light/dark and onboarding-notify-light — the space between subtext and CTA**: wrong balance / floaty spacing. Measured ~386px of empty cream on welcome (content bottom y≈345, button top y≈731) and ~420px on notify, on an 844px viewport. `StepShell`'s `justify-start` + `flex-1` is a defensible keyboard-stability choice, but at this ratio the screen reads unfinished rather than airy.
- **settings-light/dark and every `pt-safe`/`pb-safe` screen — page padding zeroed**: cramped spacing, no breathing room. Sign-out surface still `#ffffff` at y=843 (≈12px clipped); onboarding progress bar at y=0–3 with zero top clearance; welcome sign-in link 11px off the bottom edge. Cause is one line of cascade: the `.pt-safe`/`.pb-safe`/`.px-safe`/`.tap` helpers sit **outside** any `@layer` in `globals.css:232-236` and therefore beat every Tailwind utility on the same element.

Checked and **not** found: (1) default or generic gradients — every `gradient` occurrence in the tree is one of three `mask-image` scroll-edge fades, one `from-transparent to-bg` scrim over the locked memory, or Pip's own `#FFE8A0 → #FFDE7A → #F5B841` radial body plus a `#FFDE7A` glow, which is exactly the sunlight→honey glow the brand book permits and nothing else. (2) Default framework spacing — zero `p-3/p-5/px-3/py-5/gap-3` in `app/` or `components/`; the 12px/20px tell is genuinely absent. (3) Too many colours or fonts — 6 sizes, 3 families, 3 weights, confirmed in source and by the gate; the palette is the brand book plus AA-derived inks with no new hue family. (4) Generic SaaS copy — I read every button and heading string in the round; no "unlock", no "everything you need", no "journey", no "powerful". (5) Glassmorphism / gratuitous blur — exactly one blur in the codebase, `blur-[3px] saturate-50` on `MemoryDetail.tsx:53`, `aria-hidden`, and motivated: it hides locked words while keeping the date and line lengths honest.

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Wrap the four helper classes in `@layer utilities`.** `globals.css:232-236` (`.pt-safe`, `.pb-safe`, `.px-safe`, `.tap`) and `globals.css:236` (`.font-reading { line-height: 1.6 }`) are unlayered, so they outrank every Tailwind utility on the same element. One change restores the intended 32px onboarding top/bottom padding, un-clips the settings sign-out (currently white at y=843), revives `min-h-14` on `SettingRow`, and makes `leading-tight` real on the keepsake date (51px measured → 40px intended). This is a five-line fix that repairs spacing on nine screens.
2. **Give a lone photo the whole row, and give the memory card's date a non-breaking year.** `MemoryCard.tsx:38` and `MemoryDetail.tsx:113` need the `col-span-2` treatment `PhotoBubble.tsx:14` already implements — a single photo currently renders 152px wide in a 310px column with 150px of white beside it. In the same pass, replace the `, ` before the year with ` ` (or add `text-wrap: balance`) so "Monday, September 7, 2026" stops orphaning "7, 2026".
3. **Collapse the two crisis lists into one component and lock the radius namespace.** `CrisisCard.tsx:24-42` and `app/help/page.tsx:20-40` render the same five resources with different row anatomy (2-line vs 3-line) and different containers (`rounded-2xl bg-surface-2` vs `rounded-card bg-surface`) — one `<ResourceList compact>` fixes it. Then do to radii what `--text-*: initial` did to sizes: wipe the default radius namespace so `rounded-xl/2xl/3xl` stop compiling, and either use `--radius-field` for inputs or delete it (it is currently used once while two inputs ignore it).
