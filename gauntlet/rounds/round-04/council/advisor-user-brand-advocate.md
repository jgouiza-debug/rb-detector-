## Red flags

- **Generic copy — HIT.** `timeline-light-full.jpg`, the Mon Sep 7 and Sun Sep 6 memory cards: both reflections close on the identical line "Some of it good, some of it not, all of it mine." Source is the fixed `CLOSINGS` table in `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts` (three lines per mood, hash-selected — collisions across seven days are near-certain). Same file, same screen: three of seven reflections end on a closer that reuses a word already in the body ("heavy…heavy", "Quiet…Quiet"). Secondary: `app/(app)/pause/page.tsx` sets the document title to "Mindful Pause" — "mindfulness made easy" is a named brand don't (§11).
- **Wrong balance / floaty spacing — HIT.** `onboarding-welcome-light.jpg`: last line of copy ends y≈340, CTA begins y≈730 — ~390px of empty cream with Pip pinned into the top third on the brand's single most important first impression. `onboarding-notify-light.jpg` is the same shape at ~420px. `StepShell.tsx` justifies content-high for keyboard stability, which is right for the name/rhythm steps and wrong for the two steps with no input. Related: `meditation-pause-light.jpg` leaves 144px of dead ground below the last control (gate A3.void confirms).
- **Default/generic gradients — none found.** Checked every screen: the only gradients are Pip's body radial (#FFE8A0 → #FFDE7A → #F5B841, the sanctioned sunlight→honey), Pip's glow halo, and the `from-transparent to-bg` scrim on `memory-locked-paywall-*.jpg`, which is functional. No purple, no blue, no unmotivated hero wash.
- **Default framework spacing — none found.** 94.4% on 8pt with the only off-grid values being 4px micro-steps (114 of them) — permitted inside components by the taste brief. No 12/20px Tailwind tell in the report.
- **Too many colours or fonts — none found.** 3 families, 6 sizes, 3 weights; neutral family ≥55% on 21/21 screens; support accents (sky on the pause chip, blush on the help card) appear once each.
- **Repeated card/badge grids as filler — none found.** Checked `settings-light.jpg` (five identical pale-yellow icon tiles — a settings list, not filler) and `timeline-light-full.jpg`, which deliberately uses two different card shapes for "today/yesterday" vs "earlier".
- **Glassmorphism / effect because possible — none found.** The single blur (`blur-[3px] saturate-50` on the locked memory) shows me the *shape* of a day I can't read yet, which is the honest version of a lock, not decoration.

**Red-flag count: 2.**

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Fix the keepsake copy — it is the thing you are charging me for.** In `scripted.ts::synthesizeDay`: never emit a closer whose words already appear in the body (kill "It was heavy, and I'm still here." under "everything felt heavy"), and when the day's last line already lands, drop the canned closer entirely — the title already consumes that line, so the body can just be my own words. Sentence-case the whole reflection, not only its first character, so "Today was a lot honestly. work was heavy and i didn't stop once. I got through it" stops breaking case mid-keepsake. Verify by re-rendering seven seeded days and confirming no two closings match.
2. **Make the memory reveal feel like the gift the brand promises.** `animate-keepsake-in` (0.3s / 10px / scale .97) is 60ms and 4px from `animate-fade-up` (0.24s / 6px) — indistinguishable. Give the fresh card its own beat: a ~500ms settle on the card, then the mood pill and the "kept by pip" signature arriving 120ms behind the Fraunces date, all still nulled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. This is the one moment in the product that should have a shape of its own.
3. **Let the breath own the pause screen.** In `BreathingPacer.tsx`, demote "i feel ready" from `Button full size="lg"` honey to the quiet tertiary treatment "pause" currently has, and promote nothing in its place — the 32px Fraunces "breathe in…" plus "let it out, slower than you think" is enough. Retitle `PatternToggle` to a plain "switch rhythm" (drop "4-4-4-4 Box" jargon into the pressed state or a long-press) and retitle the page from "Mindful Pause" to "pause". Target: two controls visible, not four.

**Runner-ups worth a line of code each:** `BackLink` on memory detail should say "your story" like every other back link with that href, not "keepsake edition"; add a three-word "with pip+:" heading above `INCLUDED` in the subscription page so the five ticks stop contradicting "your last seven days, kept" 60px above them; gate "just paid? tap here" behind `pip_checkout_pending` in localStorage rather than showing it to everyone; and let the memory-detail date sit on one line (`formatLongDate(date, {year:true})` at 32px Fraunces wraps and orphans "7, 2026" in a 342px column).

## Notes for peers

- **Systems Designer:** the Title-Case mood labels in `lib/theme/tokens.ts` ("Heavy", "Mixed") and the Title-Case `moodLabel` strings in `scripted.ts` ("A mixed day", "Heavy but holding") are the only Title Case in an otherwise fully lowercase product. Decide whether that's a keepsake register shift or an inconsistency — I read it as a shift on the serif surfaces and an inconsistency on the timeline cards, which carry both a lowercase Fredoka header and a Title-Case pill.
- **Ergonomics & Accessibility Auditor:** please pressure-test two things the gate passed. Every chat bubble is a focusable button (34 tab stops on `thread-light.jpg`); and the sole route to the Timeline is a top-right-corner icon on an 844px viewport, against `03-design-spec.md` §2's bottom-nav instruction — the target measures 44px but the reach doesn't.
- **Interaction Engineer:** the strongest micro-interaction work in this build (`VoiceSheet.tsx` — haptics on start/stop/save, live `aria-live` transcript, graceful fall-back to a textarea when dictation is unsupported, "logged quietly" toast) does not appear in a single one of the 26 screenshots. Worth checking whether it renders as well as it reads.
- **Taste Critic:** the `WeekFlow` milestone is gated on a *complete* week and appears nowhere else in the product — I read that as a deliberate anti-streak decision rather than gamification, but you own the call.
