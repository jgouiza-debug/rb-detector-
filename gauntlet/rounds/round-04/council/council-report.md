# Council report — round 04

Chair synthesis. Five advisor reviews, five peer reviews, one machine gate report, 26 screen renders. I read the application source and spot-verified eleven screenshots at native resolution. I did not read the builder notes, the scoreboard or the ledger.

## The state of the UI

Round 4 is the round where the machine ran out of things to catch. All five Tier A gates pass, and they pass on merit, not on luck: `--text-*: initial` in `globals.css:140` wipes Tailwind's size namespace so an off-ramp type size emits no CSS at all, `lib/theme/tokens.ts` carries a `forbiddenTextPairs` registry that asserts the yellow-brand contrast traps must fail, and every failure path I opened — a dropped upload, an offline composer, a broken send — surfaces a toast or a retry rather than a dead end. The product also has a voice now. "i'm a small app, not a person who can keep you safe right now — but there are people who can, and they're there for exactly this. i'll stay here while you reach them." is the best copy in the build, and "noted quietly · no reply" on a voice note is a genuine position rather than a feature. What the machine cannot see is where this round stalls, and all of it is one layer down. The keepsake — the artifact the paywall is charging for — is still stitched: on one screenful of `timeline-light-full.jpg`, Monday Sep 7 and Sunday Sep 6 close on the *identical* sentence, "Some of it good, some of it not, all of it mine."; Monday, the day the user wrote "turns out the team was cheering for me the whole time", is tagged **Mixed** because `moodFrom`'s bright regex has no word for cheering; and today's hero card reads "Today was a lot honestly. **w**ork was heavy and i didn't stop once. **I** got through it" — three casing registers inside one keepsake. Underneath that, five helper classes in `app/globals.css` sit outside any `@layer` and therefore outrank every Tailwind utility on the same element, silently zeroing the intended page padding on nine screens and killing `leading-tight` on the one line of type the whole product builds toward. And in a state the gate structurally never renders, the composer's remove-photo button is 20×20px against a 44px floor the same file enforces everywhere else. The shell is excellent. The two surfaces that carry the product's promise — the reflection and the reveal — are the two the round did not fix.
