# Taste Critic — round 04

Could this only be Pip? The thread and the timeline: yes. The mascot is a real drawing (custom SVG, five expressions that live in the eyes, sunlight→honey radial body, blush cheeks at 0.75 opacity), the user bubbles are a confident #FFCF4D that glows on #1C1A17 instead of shouting, and the copy in the places that matter has a spine: "that's not nothing. how does it feel to be on the other side of it?", "there are 4 more days of you past the last week.", "i'm a small app, not a person who can keep you safe right now". The paywall is the best-mannered one I have seen this year: price stated, "not right now" as a plain link, "cancel anytime · export and delete stay free, always".

Then you open the keepsake and the seams show. The "reflection" is the user's own sentence with its first letter capitalised, glued to a closing line drawn from a six-row table in `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts`. On the timeline screen, "Some of it good, some of it not, all of it mine." appears twice within 300px (Mon Sep 7 and Sun Sep 6). Monday — the day "the team was cheering for me the whole time" — is tagged **Mixed** and closed with "some of it not", because `moodFrom` has no word for cheering. Tuesday reads "Today was a lot honestly. work was heavy and i didn't stop once. I got through it" — a capital T, a lowercase "work" after a full stop, a lowercase "i", then a capitalised stock sentence. That is not a friend who read the text; it is a template with a hairline crack down the middle. The brand book's own words: "Don't fake-deep. No fortune-cookie wisdom." The CLOSINGS table is a fortune-cookie jar.

Gate passes are the floor. I scored pixels.

## Red flags
- onboarding-welcome-light/dark: content block ends at y≈340, CTA starts at y≈728 — ~390px of empty cream on an 844px phone with no keyboard to justify it (StepShell `justify-start`). — wrong balance / floaty.
- settings-light, memory-card entries, composer: `rounded-2xl` (16px) rows, `rounded-xl` (12px) photo, `rounded-3xl` (24px) textarea beside `--radius-card` (20px) cards — the untouched Tailwind radius scale left standing. — default framework values.
- meditation-pause-light/dark: "4-4-4-4 Box · switch rhythm" pill; pause page `metadata.title = "Mindful Pause"`. — generic wellness-app copy.
- Checked and clean: gradients (only the Pip radial sunlight→honey, the locked-memory `to-bg` fade mask, the scroll-fade mask — all motivated); palette (21/21 screens ≥ 77% neutral); card-grid filler (the "earlier" stack and subscription list are real content); blur (locked memory `blur-[3px]` hides words on purpose; pause close `bg-surface/70` is the only translucency).

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)
1. `scripted.ts synthesizeDay`: stop gluing a stock closing onto verbatim text. Capitalise after every sentence break and normalise " i " → " I " inside `sentence()`; add "cheer|went well|nailed" to the bright regex; never reuse a closing within the last 7 rendered days (pass the previous closings in `DayInput`, or drop the table and end on the user's own last line). Until the anthropic adapter is live this is the copy people will screenshot.
2. Voice + tokens: lowercase `moodTokens[*].label` ("heavy", "mixed") and use the same string on list and detail; rename the memory-card BackLink to "your story"; collapse radii to field 14 / bubble 18 / card 20 by replacing `rounded-2xl|rounded-xl|rounded-3xl` with the tokens.
3. Pause screen: h1 in Fredoka 24px not Fraunces 32px; "i feel ready" becomes `variant="soft"` so the ring is the only bright thing; pill copy "slower rhythm" / "box rhythm"; title "a breathing moment". Then gate `animate-bubble-in` in Bubble.tsx to `message.status === "pending" || justArrived` so the thread stops popping 60 bubbles at once.
