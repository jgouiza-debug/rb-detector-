# Systems Designer — round 02

I measured. The type system here is the best-enforced I have seen in this gauntlet; the scales the
gate does not measure (radius, icon size, mascot size, vertical composition) are unenforced and have
visibly drifted. That gap is the whole review.

## Red flags

- **settings-subscription-light — "refresh status" + "upgrade to Pip+ ($4.99/mo)" buttons** (`components/settings/SubscriptionActions.tsx`): *generic copy that could belong to any app*. "refresh status" is a dev-console label promoted to a full-width `Button` in both plan states; "upgrade to" is the SaaS cousin of the "unlock" the brand book bans, and it sits in the same product as "keep it all · $4.99/mo".
- **settings-subscription-light — the gap between the "current plan" card and the CTA**: *wrong balance / floaty spacing*. ~490px of empty cream, 58% of the 844px viewport, produced by `mt-auto` with nothing authored to fill it. The gate scored this screen 0px void because it only measures ground below the last element.
- **onboarding-welcome-light/dark — 198px of empty ground below "already have pip? sign in"**: *wrong balance*. The welcome screen centres its whole stack (`justify-center`) while the four `StepShell` steps that follow anchor their CTA at the bottom, so the first screen a user ever sees is the only one that floats — and the taste brief's first best-practice note says a CTA above the fold is in the wrong place.

Checked and **not** found: (1) *default/generic gradients* — the only two gradients are `bg-gradient-to-b from-transparent to-bg` (the locked-memory scrim, single-hue, fading into the page's own ground) and the sunlight→honey radial glow behind PipMascot, which the brand explicitly permits; grepping every hex literal in `components/`+`app/` returns only brand values plus the mascot SVG — no purple, no blue-violet. (2) *default framework spacing* — every spacing utility resolves to {0,4,8,16,24,32,40}px; zero 12px, zero 20px; 6 arbitrary values total, all press transforms. (3) *too many colours or fonts* — 3 families, 6 sizes, 3 weights, 70/20/10 on 21/21 screens (the adjacent failure, nine icon sizes, is logged under A4 rather than here). (4) *repeated card grids as filler* — the five stacked `CompactCard`s under "EARLIER" are the correct form for a timeline and the two featured cards break the rhythm above them; the shape is right even though the copy inside makes it *read* as filler. (5) *glassmorphism / blur / animation because it's possible* — `backdrop-blur-md` appears once, on the sticky composer over scrolling content; `blur-[3px]` once, on the locked memory where the blur *is* the message; zero spinners exist.

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Author the vertical, then lock the radius scale.** Replace `mt-auto` on settings-subscription with real content — what Pip+ actually gives you, in the `PaywallCard`'s voice — and delete "refresh status" from the user-facing UI (or demote it to a text link inside `settings/about`). Then collapse the corner radii: keep `--radius-field`, `--radius-bubble`, `--radius-card`, `--radius-pill` and ban `rounded-2xl/3xl/xl` the same way `--text-*: initial` bans stray type sizes — the fastest visible win is `SettingRow`'s `rounded-2xl`→`rounded-card` and its `size-9` disc→`size-10`, so settings-light stops showing two card shapes in one stack.
2. **Give icons a scale and give Fraunces its role back.** Six icon steps (12/16/20/24 + the two mascot presets) instead of nine ad-hoc values; the mood icon should be one size, not 13px in pills and 15px in dots. Then drop `font-reading` from `BreathingPacer`'s h1 — set "breathe in…" in Fredoka at 24px so the serif keeps meaning "this is a memory, slow down" and nothing else. While you're in the memory card, render `formatLongDate` at 24px or with `text-wrap: balance` so "Sunday, September 6, 2026" stops orphaning "6, 2026".
3. **Fix the payoff copy and the route to it.** The `Later,` connective in `scripted.ts:86` makes seven consecutive keepsakes read as one mail-merge in every timeline screenshot — vary the join or drop it and let the two sentences stand; stop deriving compact-card titles from the first four words ("Hard day honestly, everything"). And restore a way to reach the timeline from the thumb zone — spec §2's two-item bottom nav, or at minimum move the `BookHeart` link out of the top-right corner.

## Notes for peers

- **Do not let the passing gate stand in for craft on spacing.** A3 scored 95.2% and 0 misalignments, and it *is* clean horizontally — but its void check only looks below the last element, which is exactly why it recorded "0px void" for settings-subscription, the screen with a 490px hole in the middle. Look at the middle of screens, not the bottom.
- **Interaction Engineer:** `components/ui/Skeleton.tsx:4` uses `animate-pulse`, the one `animate-*` class missing from the `prefers-reduced-motion` block in `globals.css`. The gate's reduced-motion probe visited only loaded screens, so it never saw a skeleton. Worth confirming you read it the same way before it's counted against A1.
- **Accessibility Auditor:** every chat bubble is a `<button>` (`Bubble.tsx:24`) whose only accessible name is the message text, inside `role="log"` — 35 of them on thread-light, 46 on thread-crisis. Worth a call on whether that's acceptable or noise.
- **Taste Critic:** the `Later,` template is a fixture artifact (`lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts:86`), not the production prompt (`lib/ai/prompts/synthesis.ts`). I still scored it, because it is what the rendered timeline shows — but you may want to weigh it differently.
- **User & Brand Advocate:** compare thread-light and thread-dark side by side. `--night-bubble-pip: #33302A` turns Pip's bubbles grey while the user's stay `#FFCF4D`; I read that as the companion losing its warmth in dark mode, and I'd like a second opinion on whether that's fatal or fine.
