## Red flags

- **timeline-light-full / timeline-dark-full — the six EARLIER compact cards**: generic/templated copy. Every card is `title` = first four words of its own body, repeated verbatim one line down, and every reflection carries the same hard-coded "Later," seam. Identical shapes stacked down the page is fine for a timeline; identical *sentences* is the template smell.
- **settings-subscription/light — "refresh status" (and "upgrade to Pip+ ($4.99/mo)")**: copy that could belong to any app's billing screen, in the one product whose whole thesis is voice.
- **settings-subscription/light — page balance**: ~520px of empty cream between the plan card and the CTA. Floaty, no breathing room where it's needed and all of it where it isn't.
- Checked and **not** found: default/unmotivated gradients (only Pip's permitted `sunlight → honey` radial, plus a single-hue `to-bg` scrim over the locked memory); default framework spacing (95.2% on-8pt, 4px steps confined inside components, h-11/12/14 buttons, p-6 cards); too many fonts/colours (3 families, 6 sizes, 3 weights, neutral ≥68% of pixels on every screen); glassmorphism for its own sake (the only blur is the locked keepsake, where seeing the *shape* and not the words is the point; `backdrop-blur-md` on the top bar/composer is near-invisible on cream but serves legibility over scrolling bubbles); fonts too big/small.

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Kill the "Later," template and the duplicate titles in `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts:83,86`.** Vary the connective by mood and position (or drop it and just join the two sentences), and derive the compact-card title from something the body doesn't already say — the mood label plus a noun, or nothing at all with the date carrying the row. This is the artifact the whole product exists to hand back; seven identical seams down one scroll is the single thing that made me stop believing a person was on the other end.
2. **Make settings → subscription answer "what am I buying?" and make the paywall list more than one benefit.** Put the five Pip+ lines from §3.7 into `PaywallCard` under the headline (small, `text-fg-soft`, no checkmark grid), fill the 520px hole on the subscription page with the same list plus renewal/cancel plainly stated, and rewrite "refresh status" in Pip's voice ("check again"). Right now the emotional case is strong and the practical case is missing, which is exactly why I'd close the sheet.
3. **Fix the pause screen's hierarchy and its exit.** Demote "i feel ready" from `size="lg"` primary to a ghost/soft button under the ring so the 240px breathing Pip is the only loud thing, and have that button call `POST /api/pause/complete` before routing so an early exit still earns "nice. want to tell me how that felt?". Move "switch rhythm" and the Plus 3-minute link behind the close/settings affordance — this screen should ask for one decision, not five.
