## Red flags
- timeline-light-full / timeline-dark-full: EARLIER — five identical CompactCards (date 14px / Title-Case pill / 18px serif title / 2-line clamp), each ending in "…"; repeated identical section shapes stacked as filler. "small win but i'll take it...." shows the clamp's ellipsis after a period.
- settings-subscription-light: a 100px "current plan / free" card, then ~500px of empty cream before the CTA — wrong balance, floaty spacing.
- settings-subscription-light: "current plan", "upgrade to Pip+ ($4.99/mo)", "refresh status" — generic copy that could belong to any subscription app; the only screen in the product with no Pip voice.
- memory-card-light / memory-card-dark: h1 `text-3xl` Fraunces "Sunday, September / 6, 2026" in a 294px column — font too big for its measure, year orphaned.
- Gradients: none found beyond the permitted sunlight→honey radial in PipMascot (#FFE8A0→#FFDE7A→#F5B841) and a motivated `to-bg` fade over the locked ghost card. Checked: PipMascot defs, MemoryDetail locked branch, globals.css, StepShell progress bar (solid).
- Default framework spacing: none found. Off-grid values are 4px micro-steps inside components (gate: 4px×96); no 12/20px paddings in any component read. Checked: Button, Pill, Chip, SettingRow, MemoryCard, CompactCard, Composer, StepShell.
- Blur / glass for its own sake: none found. `backdrop-blur-md` appears only on sticky TopBar and Composer over scrolling content; `blur-[3px]` only on the locked card, where it carries meaning.

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)
1. Fix the keepsake's voice in `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts synthesizeDay`: never prefix "Later, " to an entry that already begins with a conjunction; pick one case convention (keep the writer's lowercase or sentence-case everything, not "Today… i… I'm"); rotate the closing line per day so two heavy days on one screen don't both end "That counts."; derive `title` from the first clause (up to a comma/period, ≤ 28 chars) instead of four words, or drop compact titles and let the date carry. This is the product's payoff and the place the template shows most.
2. Rebuild settings-subscription as the PaywallCard pattern: Pip happy 56px, "keep your whole story", "keep it all · $4.99/mo", "not right now"; delete "refresh status" (refetch on focus instead); lowercase "pip+" everywhere; let the plan card carry content ("free · your last 7 days kept") so the page has a focal point instead of a void.
3. Let Pip react: in Bubble.tsx pass `expression="concern"` when `message.safetyLevel` is concern/crisis and `"happy"` on celebrate replies (the field and the expressions already exist). In the same pass, drop MemoryDetail's h1 to `text-2xl` or omit the year so the date sits on one line, rename the "keepsake edition" back link to "your story", and lowercase the mood pills ("heavy", "a mixed day").
