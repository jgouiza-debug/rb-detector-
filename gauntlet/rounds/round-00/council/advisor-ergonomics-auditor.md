## Red flags
- **Default framework spacing** — hit. `components/chat/Bubble.tsx:25` (`px-4 py-2.5`), `components/chat/CrisisCard.tsx:14,20,25,30` (`p-3`, `py-2` on pill buttons), `components/chat/ThreadTopBar.tsx` (`gap-2`) — all untouched Tailwind half-steps (6px/10px), matching the gate's 344×12px + 336×6px off-grid tally exactly.
- **Too many colors, no 70/20/10 discipline** — hit. `settings-light.jpg` and `thread-light.jpg`: gate measures dominant-colour share at 50.4% and 52.6% respectively, both under the 55% floor; visually the settings screen mixes cream, white, warm-red, and yellow icon chips with no clear lead colour.
- **Wrong balance (fonts too big/small)** — hit. 10 distinct type sizes (11–36px) against the brief's "type ramp of six" (taste-brief.md §4); visible as three competing display sizes in `timeline-light-full.jpg` ("Your Story" 36px h1, "Monday, September 7" 24px h3, italic subtitle at a fourth size).
- **Glassmorphism/blur used because possible** — soft hit, flagging for peer judgment. `components/chat/Composer.tsx:70`: `bg-bg/95 backdrop-blur-md` on the sticky composer bar. Minor and arguably functional (keeps the input legible over scrolling messages), but it's blur where the brand doc specifies flat surfaces only — naming it rather than silently passing it.
- **Default or generic gradients** — none found. Checked `onboarding-welcome` (light+dark), `memory-card`, `memory-locked-paywall`, `meditation-pause` for any purple/blue or multi-hue gradient; only the permitted sunlight→honey glow behind Pip appears (`onboarding-welcome-light.jpg`, `meditation-pause-light.jpg`).
- **Repeated card/badge grids as filler** — none found. The timeline's stacked cards are real distinct daily content (different dates/moods/reflections in `timeline-light-full.jpg`), not identical filler shapes.
- **Generic SaaS copy** — none found. Read every visible string across all 26 screens; nothing resembling "everything you need," "unlock your best self," or "powerful features" — copy stayed specific and personal throughout ("watching it grow feels good," "three days of fog just lifted").

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)
1. **Systemically fix sub-44px tap targets.** Add `.tap`/`min-height:44px` to `Bubble.tsx`'s message buttons (currently 40.6px, hit on 33/79 thread targets combined across light+dark+crisis) and restyle the onboarding time pickers (`app/(onboarding)/rhythm/page.tsx`) so the tappable hour/minute control fills the 44px+ label row instead of rendering the browser's intrinsic 28px-tall native widget. This single fix clears the hard cap that's holding the whole round at ≤80.
2. **Kill the timeline's horizontal scroll.** `timeline-light-full.jpg` is 644px wide against a 390px viewport (+254px), slicing the mood-filter chips and the day's reflection text mid-word. Find the unconstrained child (likely the `WeekFlow` day row or the `main` wrapper in `TimelineView.tsx`) and cap it to `max-w-full`/`overflow-x-hidden` with internal wrap. This is the app's payoff screen and the hard cap it triggers (→75) is second only to the tap-target one.
3. **Anchor single-goal screens' CTAs to the thumb zone.** `onboarding`'s `StepShell.tsx` already does this correctly (flex column, content centered, footer pinned to viewport bottom) — reuse that shell for `PaywallCard`/`MemoryDetail`'s locked state and the `settings/subscription` page, both of which currently place their one CTA near the top third (y≈224–294) with 400-600px of dead space below it in `memory-locked-paywall-light.jpg` and `settings-subscription-light.jpg`.
