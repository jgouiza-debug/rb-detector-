# Systems Designer — round 00

I measured before I looked. There is a real token layer here — `lib/theme/tokens.ts` ships a semantic palette, an `allowedPairs` contrast whitelist and a `forbiddenTextPairs` list, and `app/globals.css` defines `--radius-field/bubble/card/pill`, a three-step elevation ladder and two named easings. That is more system than most round-00 builds have. The problem is that almost nothing downstream uses it. The tokens are declared and then bypassed by raw Tailwind, and the grid is not a grid.

## Red flags

- `meditation-pause-light` / `meditation-pause-dark`: full-screen `bg-gradient-to-b from-surface to-bg` (`BreathingPacer.tsx:55`) — **unmotivated gradient**. The brand permits exactly one gradient, `sunlight → honey` behind Pip. This one is white→cream (dark: two near-identical charcoals) and it is the direct cause of that screen's 39.2% dominant-colour failure.
- `thread-light` / `thread-dark`: 12px page gutter (`TopBar`, `MessageList`, `Composer` all `px-3`) — **default framework spacing left standing**, 70 uses of the 12px step app-wide, plus a lone `p-7` (28px) on `PaywallCard`.
- `settings-light`, `help-light-full`, `timeline-light-full`: **repeated card stacks as filler** — 5 identical `SettingRow` cards, 6 identical crisis cards with the same label/title/detail/button shape, 6 identical `CompactCard`s. Same rectangle down the page on three of the app's seven surfaces.
- `memory-card-light` + `settings-subscription-light`: **floaty / wrong balance** — the keepsake card ends at y≈537 and the subscription card at y≈300, leaving ~300px and ~540px of empty cream below with nothing anchored to the bottom of an 844px screen.
- `memory-locked-paywall-light`, `timeline-light-full`, `settings-subscription-light`: **generic copy** — "Unlock with Pip+ ($4.99/mo)" and "unlock your full timeline" use the exact verb the taste brief bans; `meditation-pause` "MINDFUL PAUSE" (uppercase, tracked) and "4-4-4-4 Box · tap to switch" read as spec text, not Pip.
- App-wide: **too many type sizes** — 10 rendered, including the one-off `text-[11px]`, `text-[15px]`, `text-[17px]`. Families (3) and weights (3) are clean, so this is the only leg of the type system that is broken.

Checked and **not** found: glassmorphism-for-its-own-sake (`backdrop-blur-md` appears twice, both on sticky bars over scrolling content — justified); purple/blue "under construction" gradients (none, the only decorative gradient is the on-brand Pip body radial); font sprawl (3 families, 3 weights, both inside budget); colours mushed together (every hex traces to `lib/theme/tokens.ts`, and `forbiddenTextPairs` actively bans yellow-on-cream text).

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Fix the timeline's 644px overflow, then re-cut the header.** `timeline/light` and `/dark` render 254px wider than the viewport and clip every reflection mid-word — this alone triggers the →75 cap and destroys the payoff screen. `TimelineView` renders `<main className="mx-auto max-w-2xl px-4 py-4">` as a child of the `flex min-h-[100dvh] flex-col` wrapper in `app/(app)/layout.tsx`; give the flex item `min-w-0 w-full` and cap `max-w-2xl` behind the viewport. While in there, collapse the four competing header blocks: keep "Your Story" at 30px, move the six mood chips behind the search field, and let the first keepsake land above y≈240.
2. **Collapse the ramp to six sizes and one gutter.** Delete `text-[11px]`, `text-[15px]`, `text-[17px]` and map them to the nearest step (11→12, 15→14 for UI / 16 for reading, 17→18); that takes 10 rendered sizes to 7, and dropping `text-xl` (20px, unused on every captured screen) reaches the six. Then pick **one** page gutter — 16px — and apply it to `TopBar`, `MessageList`, `Composer`, `TimelineView`, `MemoryDetail`, `settings` and `StepShell`, which today run 12/12/12/16/16/20/24. That single change moves the on-8 share off 48.4% more than any other edit, because 12px accounts for 344 of the off-grid values.
3. **Make the tokens binding, starting with the bubble.** Change `Bubble.tsx` `py-2.5` → `py-3`: the bubble goes 40.6px → 44.6px and 16 of the 34 sub-44 targets on `thread/light` clear in one line. Then replace all 21 `rounded-2xl` with `rounded-card`, alias `rounded-full` → `rounded-pill`, normalise card padding to `p-4`/`p-6` only (kill the `p-7`), and move `Pill`'s `px-2.5 py-1` (10/4px) to `px-3 py-1.5`. That retires the 10px×218 and 6px off-grid counts and makes `settings-light` stop showing two different card radii stacked on top of each other.

## Notes for peers

- **Interaction Engineer:** the reduced-motion work is real, not claimed — `globals.css` zeroes the named keyframes rather than only shortening transitions, and the gate found 0 running animations on all 8 probes. But please confirm my read that `Bubble`, `SettingRow`, `CompactCard` and the timeline filter tabs have **zero** press states; if you agree, B3 should not go above 5.
- **Ergonomics auditor:** the design spec (§2) mandates a two-item bottom nav (Thread / Timeline). There isn't one — navigation lives in 20px top-right icons at y≈27 on `thread` and `timeline`. That is a spec deviation as well as a thumb-zone issue, and it interacts with the A2 primary-action failures.
- **Taste Critic:** the "Looking back, it felt like a lot to hold, and I got through it." repetition across all 7 memories comes from `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts:60`, the scripted fixture adapter, not from hand-written product copy. I docked B5 for it because it is what rendered, but you may want to weigh it as a fixture-quality failure rather than a voice failure.
- **Everyone:** `settings-subscription-light` appears to render with a noticeably wider page gutter (~54px) than `settings-light` (~20px) despite both pages declaring the identical `mx-auto max-w-md px-5 py-6`. I could not reconcile that from source and both captures are 390px files. Worth a second pair of eyes — it may be a capture artefact rather than a layout bug, and I did not score it.
