## C2 — Brand & emotional resonance (3/7)

Onboarding I would screenshot and send to a friend. "a place to put your thoughts down. no rules, just talk to me whenever." then, in smaller ink-soft type, "i'm a companion, not a therapist. if things ever get heavy, help is one tap away." That is warm and honest in the same breath, which is the hardest thing in this brand book to pull off. "gentle nudges, morning and evening. never nagging. you can change these anytime." — that's the no-guilt principle written as a promise instead of a claim. And "← back to pip" as the settings back-link made me smile.

Then I open thread-light, the actual product, and Pip says **"i hear you, sam."** — and eight bubbles later, **"i hear you, sam."** again. Verbatim. In one screenful. The only other thing Pip contributes is **"tell me a little more?"** That is `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts:33` pushing one fallback pair, and it is what the showcase renders. Compare the brand doc's own example — "that sounds like a lot to carry today. do you want to get into it, or just let it out?" — which exists three lines above it in the same file but only fires on the word "tired". I sent "today was a lot honestly. work was heavy and i didn't stop once" and got a canned receipt. That's a chatbot skin, not a friend.

The pixels make it worse. In thread-light my own words are saturated `user-bubble` yellow at 33.6% pixel coverage, and Pip's replies are `pip-bubble` #FFF3D1 on `cream` #FFF9ED — "i hear you, sam." is barely a different colour from the wall behind it. The screen is my own monologue shouted in yellow with Pip whispering in the margin. Pip's avatar appears once, 28px, at the left edge. The brand says "Pip is the heart of the brand. Not a logo stuck in a corner." On the home screen it is a 28px logo stuck in a corner.

## C3 — Delight with purpose (3/6)

The one moment that has to feel like a gift is the memory reveal. Brand §9: "a small, warm reveal. Make it feel like a tiny gift." What ships is `SystemMoment.tsx:13` — a grey `bg-surface-2` pill reading "your day is ready to look back on" with a Moon icon. No reveal, no stagger, no Pip.

And when I tap through, memory-card-light gives me a card signed **"synthesized by pip with care"** above this text:

> "Today I big review at work today, i was so nervous going in. Later, turns out the team was cheering for me the whole time. Looking back, it felt like a lot to hold, and I got through it."

"Today I big review at work today" is a broken sentence. It's `"Today I " + entries[0].text.slice(0,80)` (`scripted.ts:60`). And on timeline-light-full I can see all seven days at once, and **every single one ends with the identical sentence: "Looking back, it felt like a lot to hold, and I got through it."** Seven days. Including "Quiet evening, made tea" — a day I described as "felt genuinely peaceful for once" — which the card tells me was "a lot to hold". Pip put words in my mouth about my own life, and got them wrong, and signed it "with care". That is the exact failure mode the brand book calls fake-deep, and telling me it cared while showing me a Mad Lib is worse than saying nothing.

What does earn its place: the WeekFlow dots carry an icon inside the colour (cloud / sun / heart / waves), so mood reads without relying on hue and never looks like a chart — that's the How We Feel lesson landed. "noted quietly · no reply" on a voice bubble is a genuinely thoughtful mechanic. And the empty state, "your first keepsake arrives tonight.", set in the reading serif, is the best sentence in the codebase.

## C4 — Flow & friction (2/5)

Capture is two taps and the composer is pinned with a photo button, a growing textarea and a 44px mic — that half is right. Onboarding is five short screens with a top progress bar; comfortably under 60s.

Everything after that leaks. The timeline payoff does not land: on timeline-light my memory is clipped mid-word at the right edge — "work was ho", "i'm home no", "i was so ner" — because the filter row forces `main` to 644px on a 390px phone. The keepsake is literally unreadable in the shot. Navigation to it lives in the top-right corner as a 20px BookHeart icon (`ThreadTopBar.tsx`), not a bottom nav; getting to my own story requires a thumb stretch to the far corner.

The paywall is the tone problem. **"keep your whole story"** is the right headline and **"cancel anytime · your export and data stay free and yours"** is the single best trust line in the product — that's a friend. But around it: "unlock your full timeline" and "**Unlock with Pip+ ($4.99/mo)**" use the one word the taste brief bans outright and the brand book lists under don'ts. The button is the only near-black `variant="strong"` fill anywhere in the app — every warm CTA ("hey pip", "i feel ready", "upgrade to Pip+") is honey, so the buy button is by some margin the loudest object in the entire product, and it sits at the bottom of the sacred timeline. Pip is absent from the whole card. There is no dismiss — `PaywallCard.tsx` has no "not now"; on memory-locked-paywall the only exit is a small "← your story" link at the top-left, i.e. the buy button is under my thumb and the escape hatch is in the hardest corner to reach. Add the persistent "7-day window" pill parked next to "Your Story" and the 402 the screen throws in the console, and it reads as the growth team, not the friend.

Would I pay $4.99? For a full timeline of the seven identical sentences I can see in timeline-light-full — no. Fix the synthesis and I'd pay without thinking about it; the price and the cancel-anytime line are honest and the number is right.

## Red flags

- **thread-crisis/light + help-light-full: resource list** — repeated identical card shapes as filler. Six full-width cards with the same uppercase region label / name / detail / pill-button anatomy, stacked. In the thread this pushes Pip's actual sentence off-screen (gate: primary at y=-64) so a person in crisis meets a directory instead of a friend.
- **timeline-light-full "EARLIER" section** — five more identical `CompactCard` shapes stacked below two large ones, each ending in the same sentence. Reads as a template loop.
- **memory-locked-paywall (light + dark) + timeline paywall block: body copy and CTA** — banned generic copy. "unlock your full timeline" and "Unlock with Pip+". Taste brief §6: "No 'unlock'". Brand don'ts: "unlock your best self".
- **meditation-pause (light + dark): page background** — unmotivated gradient. `bg-gradient-to-b from-surface to-bg` full-bleed (`BreathingPacer.tsx:55`). The only permitted gradient is `sunlight → honey` behind Pip. It is subtle, but it is not the sanctioned one, and it makes the pause screen *brighter* than the app when the spec asks for "dimmed warm background".
- **timeline-light subtitle + meditation-pause phase copy** — fake-deep / fortune-cookie. "quiet reflections quietly kept" (a phrase that says nothing twice) and "filling softly, like warm morning light". Brand §11: "No fake-deep. No fortune-cookie wisdom."
- **memory-card-light-full, settings-subscription-light, memory-locked-paywall-light: page bottoms** — wrong balance / floaty. ~300px, ~550px and ~450px of empty ground under a single card, on a 844px viewport. Onboarding-name-light has ~280px between the input and the CTA.
- **settings-dark: "get help now" card** — muddy colour. The gate names untokenised `#584838` (8.2%) and `#A87878` (0.3%) on this screen; the blush warmth turns to brown-pink sludge in dark, and the yellow icon chips that carry the light version vanish entirely, so dark is an inversion rather than a second design.
- **Whole app: type ramp** — 10 distinct sizes (11/12/14/15/16/17/18/24/30/36) against a ramp of six. 15, 16, 17 and 18 all coexist; that's decisions nobody made.

Checked and **not** found: no purple/blue "under construction" gradient anywhere; no glassmorphism or decorative blur (the one `bg-surface/70` is on the pause close button, which is functional); no default Tailwind card-grid landing layout; font families are 3 and weights are 3, exactly the brand three-role system; no red-alert mood colours — "Heavy" is warm grey-cream, never alarm red; no shame-streak, no counter guilt, no "you haven't checked in".

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Make the memory a memory.** Kill `"Today I " + rawText.slice(0,80)` and the shared closing sentence in `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts:60`. Even the offline fixture must produce a different last line per mood and a first sentence that is grammatical — seven cards ending "Looking back, it felt like a lot to hold, and I got through it." on timeline-light-full is the reason I wouldn't pay. Then drop "synthesized by pip with care" for something that doesn't advertise the machine — the tiny Pip avatar alone is the signature the brand asked for.
2. **Give Pip more than one line, and let it be seen.** Widen `scripted.ts` past the single "i hear you, {name}. / tell me a little more?" fallback so no two consecutive replies repeat, and raise Pip's bubble off the background — `pip-bubble` #FFF3D1 on `cream` #FFF9ED is a 1-step difference; give it a hairline `ring-1 ring-line` or step the surface, so Pip's voice reads as loud as mine. Bump bubble padding from `py-2.5` to `py-3.5` while you're in there and 40.6px becomes 48px, clearing the A2 cap on the app's most-touched element.
3. **Re-tone the paywall and un-clip the timeline.** Replace both instances of "unlock" ("keep your full timeline, deeper conversations, and shareable keepsake cards" / "keep it all — Pip+, $4.99/mo"), make the CTA honey rather than the only near-black fill in the product, put Pip where the `BookHeart` glyph is, and add a real "not now" under the button. Separately, add `min-w-0` to the filter row's flex container at `TimelineView.tsx:89` — that one class removes the +254px sideways scroll that is currently chopping my own memories off the right edge of the payoff screen.

## Notes for peers

- The screenshots are rendered against `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts` (the deterministic offline adapter); `lib/adapters/ai/anthropic.ts` also exists. I have scored the pixels as instructed, but the systems/interaction advisors should note that the copy defects in the timeline and memory card originate in a *fixture*, not in a component — the fix is one file, and it is the highest-leverage change in the round.
- Interaction Engineer: please verify whether the `pending` synthesis poll (`TimelineView.tsx:52`) renders any skeleton, and whether `animate-bubble-in` staggers across a multi-bubble Pip reply or fires all at once — I can't tell from stills.
- Systems Designer: the casing split is systematic, not incidental — `MOOD_FILTERS` (TimelineView.tsx:15-22), `h1 "Your Story"` (:75) and the memory-detail tab labels are the only Title Case in an otherwise lowercase product. Worth one token-level ruling.
- Ergonomics: every chat bubble being a `<button>` that only toggles a timestamp inflates the button count on the thread to ~34 and is the bulk of the 67 undersized targets. Consider whether that affordance should exist at all.
- A2's "primary off-screen at landing" on timeline is the "Unlock with Pip+" button at y=640 — worth deciding whether a paywall CTA should count as a screen's primary action at all.
