## Red flags
- **Default gradients** — borderline, not counted. `PipMascot.tsx` radial `#FFE8A0→#FFDE7A→#F5B841` is the permitted sunlight→honey glow. `BreathingPacer.tsx` `bg-gradient-to-b from-surface to-bg` (white→cream, visible in meditation-pause-light.jpg as a pale top band) is a page-wash gradient with no motivating element; keep an eye on it.
- **Default framework spacing** — HIT. Gate: 12px ×344, 20px ×52. `MessageList` `px-3 py-4`, `Composer` `px-3 py-2`, `MemoryCard` `p-5`, `CrisisCard` `p-3`, settings `px-5`. Thread, timeline and settings all sit on untouched Tailwind steps.
- **Too many colors/fonts** — not a hit. 3 families, 3 weights. Mood hues (sunlight/sky/blush/sage/line/pip-bubble) are motivated. Palette proxy fails are surface-vs-cream ratio, not extra hues.
- **Repeated card grids / identical shapes stacked** — HIT. timeline-light-full.jpg: WeekFlow card, search pill, two MemoryCards, "EARLIER" then six identical CompactCards (date / mood pill / title / two lines), then a PaywallCard — eleven white rounded boxes on cream. help-light-full.jpg: six identical cards (REGION / Name / detail / pill button). settings-light.jpg: five identical rows. Nothing on the timeline is not a card.
- **Generic copy** — HIT. "Unlock with Pip+ ($4.99/mo)" and "unlock your full timeline, deeper conversations, and shareable keepsake cards." (`PaywallCard.tsx`; brief: no "unlock"); "Search memories, words, or feelings…" (timeline); "refresh status" (settings-subscription-light.jpg); "synthesized by pip with care" (memory-card); "quiet reflections quietly kept" (timeline tagline, fortune-cookie); "MINDFUL PAUSE" eyebrow + "0:01 / 1:30" media-player clock + "4-4-4-4 Box · tap to switch" (meditation-pause: wellness-app jargon on a screen meant to slow you down).
- **Glassmorphism/blur because possible** — HIT (minor). `TopBar` `bg-bg/90 backdrop-blur-md` and `Composer` `bg-bg/95 backdrop-blur-md`: at 90–95% opacity the blur is invisible in every screenshot and costs compositing on a phone.
- **Wrong balance** — HIT. memory-card-light.jpg: Fraunces 30px date wraps, "2026" orphans on its own line, lower 45% of the screen is empty cream. memory-locked-paywall-light.jpg: card ends at y≈395 of 844, rest is void. settings-subscription-light.jpg: one card, two buttons, 70% empty. timeline-light.jpg: reflection text runs off the right edge.

Count: 5 hits (spacing, card stacks, copy, blur, balance).

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)
1. **Make the timeline read like pages, not a dashboard.** Drop "Your Story" to a lowercase 24px Fredoka eyebrow or remove it; kill the WeekFlow card border (dots on bare cream); collapse search + filters into one row that appears on scroll-up; render the top memory as full-bleed Fraunces text on cream with no card; fix the 644px overflow. Then fix the reflection template: no fixed closing sentence, no "Today I today". This alone moves B1, B5, C1, C3, C4.
2. **Remove the banned words and the Title Case.** "Unlock with Pip+" → "keep it all · $4.99/mo"; "Your Story" → "your story"; "All Memories / Keepsake Card / Raw Entries" → lowercase; delete the `Sparkles` icon; replace "synthesized by pip with care" with the tiny Pip signature alone. Retheme `Button` `strong` for dark (`bg-cream text-ink` or honey) so the paywall CTA exists on night.
3. **Let the pause screen be one thing.** Remove "MINDFUL PAUSE", the "0:01 / 1:30" clock and the countdown digit; let the ring plus "breathe in…" carry timing; move "4-4-4-4 Box" behind a single quiet toggle or into settings.
