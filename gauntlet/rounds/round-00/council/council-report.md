# Council report — round 00 (baseline)

Chair synthesis of five advisor reviews, five peer reviews, the machine gate report, and my own spot-verification of eleven screenshots. Builder notes, scoreboard and ledger not read.

## State of the UI

Pip in round 00 is two products stitched together. The onboarding five and the mascot are genuinely authored: one decision per screen, a hand-drawn SVG character with five real expressions, honest skip links, and a voice ("so i can gently check in", "i'm a companion, not a therapist") nobody else would write. Behind it sits a real token layer — a semantic palette, three type families, three weights, named easings, a complete `prefers-reduced-motion` branch that the gate confirms actually stops every animation. Then the product proper arrives and the system stops being enforced. The payoff screen renders 644px wide inside a 390px phone and guillotines the user's own memories mid-word ("work was ho…"); every one of seven daily reflections ends with the identical sentence "Looking back, it felt like a lot to hold, and I got through it.", including a Bright day about a recipe; Pip says "i hear you, sam." twice in one screenful; every chat bubble is a 40.6px tap target; spacing sits on four different page gutters and only 48.4% of the 8pt grid; ten type sizes are in play against a ceiling of six; and the buy button — "Unlock with Pip+", the one verb the brief bans — is the loudest object in the app. The craft is present in the parts nobody has to use yet, and absent in the loop the product is for.

## Red flags

Union of everything raised, each verified by the chair against the gate report and the screenshots.

1. **`meditation-pause` (light + dark): page background** — unmotivated gradient. `BreathingPacer.tsx:55` paints a full-bleed `bg-gradient-to-b from-surface to-bg`. Verified in `meditation-pause-light.jpg` as a white-to-cream vertical wash; the brand permits one gradient only, sunlight→honey behind Pip. It is the direct cause of `meditation-pause/dark`'s 39.2% dominant-colour failure. (Two advisors reported "no gradients found"; refuted by the pixels and the source line.)
2. **App-wide, most visible on `thread` and `settings`: page spacing** — default framework spacing left standing. 12px×344, 6px×336, 10px×218 off-grid values from untouched Tailwind half-steps (`Bubble.tsx px-4 py-2.5`, `MessageList gap-1.5`, `CrisisCard p-3`), plus four different page gutters and a lone `p-7`. Chair-verified: `settings-light` gutter ≈x20 vs `settings-subscription-light` ≈x55 on two pages declaring the same class string.
3. **`timeline-light-full` + `settings-light`: card stacks** — repeated identical shapes as filler. Eleven white rounded boxes on cream down the timeline, of which the "EARLIER" run is five identical `CompactCard`s (date / mood pill / title / two lines), then a paywall card; settings is five identical rows. Verified. (The `help` / `thread-crisis` resource directory repeats the same shape too, but there the repetition is the content — six real hotlines — so it is excluded from this flag.)
4. **`memory-locked-paywall` (both themes), `timeline`, `meditation-pause`: copy** — generic / banned. "Unlock with Pip+ ($4.99/mo)" and "unlock your full timeline, deeper conversations, and shareable keepsake cards" use the one verb the brief bans; "MINDFUL PAUSE" in tracked uppercase, "0:01 / 1:30" and "4-4-4-4 Box · tap to switch" are spec voice on the screen meant to slow a person down; "synthesized by pip with care" is a machine describing itself. All verified in the captures.
5. **`timeline-light-full`: every memory card** — templated content, the mail-merge pattern. All 7 reflections end on "Looking back, it felt like a lot to hold, and I got through it.", including the Bright day ("Tried the new recipe") and the Calm one ("Quiet evening, made tea"); each opens on the ungrammatical "Today I " + raw text. Chair-verified across all seven cards.
6. **`settings-subscription-light`, `memory-locked-paywall` (both), `memory-card-light`: page bottoms** — wrong balance, floaty. Subscription is one card + one button + a text link ending at y≈300 with ~540px of empty cream below on an 844px screen; the paywall card ends at y≈400 with ~440px of void; the keepsake ends at y≈537. Verified.
7. **App-wide: type ramp** — wrong balance. 10 rendered sizes (11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 24, 30, 36px) against a ramp of six; 15, 16, 17 and 18 all coexist. Gate-measured.
8. **`settings-dark`: "get help now" card** — muddy colour. Chair-verified: the blush warmth becomes a brown-mauve slab, and the gate names it as untokenised `#584838` at 8.2% of pixels plus `#A87878`. The yellow icon chips that carry the light version disappear entirely in dark.

**Dropped after peer refutation:**
- *Glassmorphism / blur used because possible* (`TopBar bg-bg/90 backdrop-blur-md`, `Composer bg-bg/95`) — raised by two advisors, judged functional by three (a sticky bar over scrolling content), and at 90–95% opacity no blur is visible in any of the 26 captures. Not a hit.
- *Dark paywall CTA unthemed and "nearly invisible" (`#2B2620` on `#26231F`)* — refuted with screenshot evidence by three peers and retracted by its own author. Chair spot-check of `memory-locked-paywall-dark.jpg`: the plate is low-separation but the label is bold white and plainly legible, and the gate measures 0 contrast failures on that screen. Not a flag. (The related, surviving point — that this is the only near-black `strong` fill in an app of honey CTAs — is scored under C4, not flagged.)

**Final red-flag count: 8.**

## Top 3 fixes for round 01

Ordered by points unlocked per unit of work: the lower cap first, then the higher cap, then the biggest Tier B/C gap.

1. **Lift the →75 cap: stop the timeline scrolling sideways, and stop the paywall logging a 402.** Screen: `timeline` (light + dark) and `memory-locked-paywall`. Element: the mood-filter row inside `<main className="mx-auto max-w-2xl px-4 py-4">` in `TimelineView.tsx` (the flex child needs `min-w-0 w-full`), and `MemoryDetail`'s fetch of `/api/memory/:date`, which already treats 402 as an expected locked state but still lets the failed request reach the console. Target: `main` measures **≤390px** at a 390px viewport with **0px** horizontal overflow on both themes, every reflection wrapping instead of clipping; **0 console errors** across all 21 renders. This is one class and one caught response, and it is the only thing holding the round at ≤75 — it also repairs B1, C3 and C4, because the payoff screen currently destroys its own payoff.
2. **Lift the →80 cap: make every target 44px and put the primary action in the thumb zone.** Screen: `thread` (all three variants), `onboarding-rhythm`, then `memory-locked-paywall` / `settings-subscription` / `help` / `timeline`. Element: `Bubble.tsx` `py-2.5` → `py-3.5` (**40.6px → 48px**, clearing ~33 of the 67 undersized targets in one line); the native `<input type="time">` rows in `app/(onboarding)/rhythm/page.tsx` (117.9×28 → **≥44px** tappable height); the "already have pip? sign in" link on welcome (342×20 → **≥44px**); and the single CTA on the four out-of-zone screens, re-shelled with the bottom-anchored `StepShell` pattern onboarding already gets right. Target: **0/210** interactive targets under 44×44, and **21/21** screens with a ≥44px primary action on screen at landing with its centre below y≈500.
3. **Make the memory a memory, and give Pip more than one line.** Screen: `timeline`, `memory-card`, `thread`. Element: `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts:60` — kill `"Today I " + text.slice(0,80)` and the fixed closing sentence so no two of the seven cards share a last line and a Bright day never reads as "a lot to hold"; widen the reply set at `scripted.ts:33` past the single "i hear you, {name}. / tell me a little more?" pair so no two consecutive Pip bubbles repeat; raise `pip-bubble #FFF3D1` off `cream #FFF9ED` with a hairline `ring-1 ring-line` so Pip's voice reads as loud as the user's 33.6%-coverage yellow. Target: **0 of 7** memory cards sharing a closing sentence, **0** verbatim Pip repeats in one screenful, and both instances of "unlock" replaced (paywall headline and body) with a honey CTA and a real "not now". This is the largest remaining Tier B/C block — B5 (4.5/8), C2 (4.5/7), C3 (3/6), C4 (3/5) all move on one fixture file plus one token.
