## Red flags

- **Wrong balance — floaty in the middle, cramped at the edge.** `onboarding-welcome-light`: a 376px empty band between the last body line and the "hey pip" CTA; `onboarding-name-light`: 464px; `settings-subscription-light`: 212px; `meditation-pause-light`: 156px of dead ground below the "pause" button. Simultaneously, 0px below the CTA on `settings-light`, `settings-subscription-light`, `memory-locked-paywall-light` and `onboarding-name-light` (last ink y=843 of 844), and 0px above the progress bar on onboarding steps 1–4 (first ink y=0). One root cause, both symptoms.
- **Default framework spacing (radius form).** `settings-light` SettingRow and the `onboarding-name-light` input use `rounded-2xl` — Tailwind's untouched 16px default — while the app's own `--radius-field` (14px) is used exactly once anywhere. `rounded-3xl` (24px, composer field) and `rounded-xl` (12px, memory-detail entry photo) are the same story.
- **Default/generic gradients — none found.** Checked every `gradient` occurrence in `app/` and `components/`: two radialGradients inside PipMascot (sunlight→honey, brand-permitted) and one `from-transparent to-bg` scrim on the locked memory. No purple, no blue, no multi-hue, no unmotivated fills.
- **Too many colours or fonts — none found.** 3 families, 3 weights, 6 sizes, all structurally enforced; gate's 70/20/10 proxy passes 23/23 with neutral dominance 45–96%.
- **Repeated card/badge grids as filler — none found.** Checked `timeline-light-full` and `timeline-dark-full` end to end: the five CompactCards are a date list, each with distinct copy and a distinct mood pill, and no section shape is duplicated to fill space.
- **Generic copy — none found.** Read every string on all 23 screens. No "everything you need", "built for", "powerful features", "unlock", "journey".
- **Glassmorphism / gratuitous blur or animation — none found.** One blur in the app (`blur-[3px]` on the locked memory), which is the screen's entire premise. No `backdrop-filter` anywhere.

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Give the safe-area utilities a floor.** Add `--pt-safe-min: 1.5rem; --pb-safe-min: 1.5rem;` to `:root` in `globals.css`. `.pt-safe`/`.pb-safe` currently resolve to 0px and, sitting later in the utilities layer at equal specificity, silently null the `py-8`/`py-6` on StepShell, every settings page, `/help` and MemoryDetail. One declaration restores 32/24px of top and bottom gutter on nine screens and stops the CTA touching the edge. This is the single highest-leverage line in the round.
2. **Retire the Tailwind default radii.** Swap `rounded-2xl` → `rounded-field` (15 sites: SettingRow, AccountEditor, RhythmEditor, DangerZone, SystemMoment, name input, sign-in), `rounded-3xl` → `rounded-card` (Sheet, composer textarea), `rounded-xl` → `rounded-card` (Skeleton, `MemoryDetail.tsx:124`). That takes the app from six rendered radii to four role-named ones and makes `--radius-field` mean something. While you're in `MemoryDetail.tsx:124`, add `w-full` so the entry photo stops ending 70px short of its own card's content column.
3. **Put the micro-label column on the page column.** Drop `px-2` from the five uppercase `<h2>`s ("you", "how pip reaches you", "your words", "earlier", "what i said, that day") and from `WeekFlow`'s `<section>`. They currently sit at x=24 while the h1 is at 16 and the cards they label are at 16 — the labels align to nothing on three separate screens. Then collapse the icon ladder to four steps (16/20/24/28) and the mood glyph to one size, so the same Sun doesn't render at 13, 15 and 15 on one screen.

## Notes for peers

- **Interaction Engineer:** every message bubble in `Bubble.tsx` is a `<button type="button" title="tap for the time">`, so a screen reader announces ~60 scrollback messages as buttons. It passes axe and it's how the timestamp reveal works, but check whether that's the right affordance. Also: `Toast` auto-dismisses on a bare 3600ms `setTimeout` with no dismiss control, and `TimelineView` polls synthesis at 3/8/15s and then stops — verify there's no dead state if synthesis takes longer.
- **Ergonomics Auditor:** the gate's "primary in thumb zone" passes, but I measured 0px of clearance below the primary CTA on four screens because `--pb-safe-min` is undefined. On a notched iPhone `env()` covers it; in a browser tab, on Android, or in landscape it does not. Worth your own read.
- **Taste Critic:** confirm my read on two judgment calls — Fraunces on the pause screen ("breathe in…", 32px) when the brand reserves the reading serif for memory surfaces, and the lucide `Contrast` glyph standing in for the "mixed" mood among Sun/Waves/Cloud/Heart/Sprout.
- **Brand Advocate:** the mood badge is lowercase "bright" on the timeline card and "Bright & warm" on the memory detail. One of those is the brand voice and one isn't; you should pick which.
