# Taste Critic — round 05

Could this only be Pip? Mostly yes, and that is the best thing I can say about this round. The mascot is a hand-drawn SVG with a sprout and blush cheeks, not an emoji. The voice is real: "kept by pip", "2 moments kept", "what i said, that day", "your words" as the name of the data section, "i'm a small app, not a person who can keep you safe right now". The paywall says "keep it all · $4.99/mo" over "not right now" over "cancel anytime · export and delete stay free, always" — that is the best paywall copy I have read this year. Then I read `lib/adapters/ai/scripted.ts` and looked at the seven keepsakes again, and the seams showed.

## Red flags
- timeline-light-full and timeline-dark-full: Thu Sep 3 CompactCard — closing "I'll take it, all of it." under title "Small win but i'll take it" — generic/machine copy (a fixed-pool closer echoing the body it was meant to avoid; `scripted.ts:92` threshold bug).
- memory-locked-paywall light and dark: locked card `blur-[3px] saturate-50` under `bg-gradient-to-b from-transparent to-bg` (MemoryDetail.tsx) — blur used as a tease; the date stays legible through it, which is the freemium-template move, not honesty.
- onboarding-welcome, onboarding-notify: copy ends at ≈y340 / ≈y310, CTA top at ≈y728 / ≈y725 — roughly 390–415px of empty cream on a 844px screen — floaty spacing. Anchoring the CTA is right; leaving a 140px Pip to hold 46% of the screen is not.
- SettingRow / name input / rhythm rows / SystemMoment (`rounded-2xl`), composer textarea and Sheet (`rounded-3xl`), Skeleton and detail images (`rounded-xl`) — untouched Tailwind radii alongside the three custom radius tokens — default framework values left standing.
- Checked and clean: no unmotivated gradients (Pip's radial `#FFE8A0→#FFDE7A→#F5B841` is the one permitted sunlight→honey glow); palette holds 70/20/10 on all 23 screens; no card-grid filler (the "earlier" stack is content); no "unlock/journey/powerful" anywhere.

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)
1. Put the user's last line back in the keepsake. In `scripted.ts` either render `title` on MemoryCard/MemoryDetail as the card's headline (Fraunces 24, date drops to a 14px caption) or fold it into `reflection` before the closer. Today's card should read "…i didn't stop once. But i made it through, and i'm home now." — the person's own ending, and only then, if at all, a closer. Also drop the echo threshold at line 92 from 5 letters to 4 and include the title's words in `bodyWords`.
2. Pick one casing rule for the keepsake and hold it. Either leave the user's text exactly as typed (lowercase is the brand) or capitalise the "i" too. "Today was a lot honestly. Work was heavy and i didn't stop once." is currently neither. Same fix retires "Bright & warm" → "bright & warm".
3. Memory detail date: drop the year (the timeline never shows it) or set it at 24px so "Monday, September 7" sits on one line; and give the two timeline card types one grammar — make MemoryCard tappable like CompactCard, same pill anatomy on both.
