## Red flags

- `onboarding-welcome-light` / `onboarding-welcome-dark`: **wrong balance — floaty spacing.** The last subtext line ends ≈y345 and the "hey pip" button starts ≈y731 on an 844px screen: **~386px of empty cream** in the middle of the brand's first handshake. `StepShell.tsx` justifies content-top for keyboard stability, which is right on the *name* step and unearned on welcome and notify (~360px) — the same shell also leaves ~250px on `onboarding-focus-light` and ~200px on `settings-subscription-light` (the gate's lowest 8pt share, 81.4%).

Checked and **not** found: default/generic gradients — the only two are the motivated sunlight→honey glow behind Pip (`onboarding-welcome-dark`, `meditation-pause-dark`) and the transparent→bg scrim over the locked card. Default framework spacing — 95.1% on 8pt, only 12px×4 off-grid. Too many colours/fonts — 3 families, 3 weights, 6 sizes, neutral family ≥55% on 23/23. Card-grid filler — the "EARLIER" rows repeat one shape but each holds distinct content, and the timeline deliberately splits hero cards from compact rows rather than flattening everything. Generic SaaS copy — I read every visible string; the only off-voice ones are the Title Case "Bright & warm" pill and the SR label "mark as resonated". Glassmorphism/blur for its own sake — the blur on `memory-locked-paywall` is the locked content itself, which is the honest way to do it.

## Top 3 fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Fix the keepsake's voice, then its metadata.** In `scripted.ts`, extend `sentence()` to also uppercase the standalone pronoun (`/\bi\b/` → `I`, and `i'm/i'll/i've/i'd`), so `memory-card-light` stops reading "Big review at work today, i was so nervous going in. I want to remember this one." Then give the source entries real clock times instead of the seed's "1:54 AM", and stop printing "2 moments kept" identically on every card (drop the count when it's the same everywhere, or say "two things you said"). This is the single artifact the whole $4.99 rests on.
2. **Put the timeline where a thumb can reach it, and name it.** Today it's an unlabelled BookHeart at ≈x304,y28. Either ship the two-item bottom nav the spec asks for (thread / your story), or at minimum put a labelled pill in the thread — the memory-ready system moment already proves the pattern with "🌙 your day is ready to look back on". Nobody pays for a payoff they haven't seen.
3. **Make the paywall worth the tap and keep its promise.** Move the five benefit lines from `settings-subscription` onto the `PaywallCard` itself, add the founder price, and either build the share action that "a keepsake card for each day, yours to share" sells or cut that line. Keep every word of the current tone — "not right now" and "export and delete stay free, always" are the best copy in the build.
