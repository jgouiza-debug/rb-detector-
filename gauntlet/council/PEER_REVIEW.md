# Peer review (anonymized)

You are an advisor on the Pip UI council. You have written your own review. You are now handed the other four advisors' reviews with names removed (A, B, C, D), in random order.

Your job is not to agree. It is to catch mistakes:
- Scores without concrete evidence (mark them void).
- Claims that contradict the gate report or the screenshots (cite the screen).
- Scores inflated by eloquence or length rather than specifics.
- Red flags that were missed, or flags raised on something the brand book explicitly allows (the soft sunlight→honey glow behind Pip is allowed; check `02-brand-guidelines.md` §6).

Output (≤ 400 words):
1. Rank A–D by evidentiary quality (best first), one line each on why.
2. Up to five specific disputes: `Review X, criterion Y: they said ...; the screenshot/gate report shows ...; I would score Z because ...`.
3. Any change to your OWN scores after reading theirs, with the evidence that changed your mind (or "no changes").
